Advanced

You're here when Intermediate variations are easy at the top of the rep range (about 10–12 good reps), so you move to harder variations: pike or advanced push-ups, weighted or advanced pull-ups, weighted Bulgarian split squats, and L-sits or weighted hanging raises; daily protein is 150 g+; and steps are a consistent 10k.

Protein: about 150 g a day
Steps: 10k daily, consistently (up to 10k–12k is fine)
Schedule & rules

Same schedule, session length, logging, rest, bracing, and 6–8 week block as Beginner. Keep RPT on the first two exercises of each day.

What changes:

  • Progress by making the variation harder (weight, angle, or taking work off one limb), not by chasing 20+ reps.
  • For pull-ups, once you can do 3×6–8, add weight, starting small — about 2.5–5 kg, or even ~1 kg in a bag.
  • For dips, the plan's progression once bodyweight is easy is to add weight, not to remove an arm.
  • For BSS, Advanced is weighted after 3×12 (at about 3×10–12, hold dumbbells instead of adding reps).
  • Dumbbell work, if you use it, still moves up at 3×8 in a 4–8 rep range.
Upper day

Same five slots; harder or loaded variations.

Slot 1

Pike push-up (RPT)

Sets/reps: 3×6–10 RPT; use when intermediate push-ups are easy at 10–12 reps
Equipment: none

Hips high, head travels forward of the hands. Pair with advanced push-up variations when this is easy.

Slot 1

Advanced push variations (RPT)

Sets/reps: 3×6–10 RPT, once 12+ reps of the intermediate versions are easy
Equipment: none; optional box or ball for feet-elevated / one-arm path

Harder push variations. Don't chase 20+ regular push-ups.

Slot 2

Weighted pull-ups (RPT)

Sets/reps: Add load once bodyweight 3×6–8 is solid; start small (~1 kg, or 2.5–5 kg)
Equipment: pull-up bar plus a backpack, belt, or bag

Use the unweighted pull-up as the form reference.

Slot 2

Advanced pull variations (RPT)

Sets/reps: 3×6–10 RPT, once 12+ bodyweight reps are easy
Equipment: pull-up bar

Harder pull variations, including a one-arm path by holding a wrist or a lower grip.

Same form as Intermediate.

Inverted rows (feet elevated, then loaded, then one-arm path)

Sets/reps: 3×6–10
Equipment: bar; box to elevate the feet; optional bag or vest

Same movement, harder setup.

Slot 5

Biceps curls (superset with overhead triceps extensions)

Sets/reps: 3×6–10, or 4–8 if loaded with dumbbells (move up at 3×8)

Daily movement / steps

Same walking-first rule as Beginner. The top of the ramp is 10k steps a day, consistently. You can also build to 10k–12k, with consistency over spikes.

Sprints are the second cardio option, for muscle and athleticism, on rest days, not as a replacement for walking. Sprint protocol: 6–10 s sprints, 3 min rest, working sets at a 7/10, 8/10, then 9/10 effort. Only if your doctor has cleared hard cardio. If not, keep walking.

Target: 150 g+ a day, after holding 130 g.

The staple-meal pattern, fasting window, and the avoid / ask-the-doctor list are the same as Beginner. 150 g in a 3-hour window is a lot of food; keep it to two servings and lean sources, and ask your doctor if that amount or the window needs to change.

Calories

Same method as Beginner. A consistent 10k steps can raise daily burn; trust the morning-weight trend over 2–3 weeks and stay inside the 300–500 deficit. If you are already at a healthy weight and the doctor agrees, the plan's alternative is maintenance or a small surplus rather than forcing a cut below 2,000 kcal. Still no personal number in this package.

Setting calories:

  1. Find your maintenance with a TDEE calculator, using your weight, height, age and activity level. Most men come out at 2,500–2,700. (The Mayo Clinic calculator is one option.)
  2. Subtract 400. A range of 300–500 is fine; never go more than 500 below. That number is your daily target.
  3. Check it against your morning-weight trend over 2–3 weeks. If your weight isn't falling, lower the target, staying inside the 300–500 range.
  4. If the target comes out below 2,000 kcal, the plan says to build muscle at maintenance or a small surplus instead of cutting. Current weight, height and age are not in this package, so there is no personal calorie number here. Example only, not your numbers: maintenance 2,600 gives a target of 2,200. The calorie rule overrides food rules: surplus = gain, deficit = lose. Pair any cut with the 3×/week lifting so losses are not mostly muscle.
Sleep

Same as Beginner.

Sleep basics:

  • Go to bed at a fixed time (for example, wind down at 10 PM and be in bed at 11 PM; pick your own fixed clock).
  • Keep your phone out of the bedroom.
  • Get morning sunlight.
  • No intense exercise late at night.
  • Screens off about an hour before bed. Trackers are fine if you don't obsess over them.
Optional extrasOptional

Same list as Beginner. At this level the plan's harder lower-body extras (full pistol squats, Nordics) are more realistic, but they still do not replace BSS, RDL, or hip thrusts on the main-routine days.

These are substitute exercises and the 4 key lower-body progressions. They are not required slots in the main routine. Use them only as substitutes or add-ons, and don't pile them onto a session until the five main movements are habitual.

ATG split squat

Sets/reps: Optional warm-up or accessory; not a main-routine slot
Equipment: none, or a support to hold

Knee far over toes, deep range, heel down. Also a lower-body warm-up.

Squat to pistol-squat progression

Sets/reps: Build toward 10 unassisted pistols; stay in a strength range
Equipment: a seat or box for the early steps

Chair/box and assisted pistols first.

Pistol squat

Sets/reps: Goal: 10 unassisted
Equipment: none

Full single-leg squat, arms forward as a counterbalance. Does not replace BSS on main-routine days.

Nordic curls

Sets/reps: Slow lowers; band assistance is fine
Equipment: something heavy to anchor the heels; optional band

Straight hips, lower as slowly as possible. Substitute exercise; not a main-routine slot.

Good mornings

Sets/reps: 3×6–10, light load
Equipment: none, or a light bag

Hip hinge, neutral spine.

Back extensions

Sets/reps: 3×6–10
Equipment: bench or table edge

Hinge at the hips, squeeze glutes at the top, don't hyperextend. Related hamstring and glute-chain demo, not the Jefferson curl.

Tibialis raises

Sets/reps: Controlled, slow
Equipment: a wall

Back to the wall, lift the toes.

Patrick step-ups

Sets/reps: Slow, controlled
Equipment: a step or low box

Heel-elevated step-down, knee over toes. Knee-health accessory.

Lateral raises

Sets/reps: Higher reps are fine (up to 20)
Equipment: light dumbbells, a loaded bag, or two books

Lead with elbows, to shoulder height, pinky level with the thumb. Not part of the main upper day; optional accessory.
