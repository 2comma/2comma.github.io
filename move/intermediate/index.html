Intermediate

You're here when full push-ups, chin-ups or pull-ups, and full Bulgarian split squats are your working sets (not just holds and mini ranges); after you've held 100 g of protein a day for a month and are moving to 130 g; and while steps are ramping through 6k and 8k toward 10k.

Protein: about 130 g a day
Steps: Ramp through 6k and 8k toward 10k, about +1k every 2 weeks after the 5k base
Schedule & rules

Same schedule, session length, logging, rest, bracing, and 6–8 week block as Beginner. Keep the Upper/Lower alternation and RPT on the first two exercises of each day.

What changes:

  • The 3×6–10 rule still applies. Progress the variation at 3×10 good form. For Bulgarian split squats, Intermediate is 3 sets of 12 ("Key Lower Body Exercises" PDF).
  • Holds and negatives are no longer the main work, except as a back-off set on a hard day.
  • Dips are in the rotation (no more automatic skip).
  • If you add dumbbells, switch those loaded moves to the gym scheme: 4–8 reps, move up at 3×8 ("Routine Explained", 3:47–3:52 and 10:29–10:36).
  • Equipment still follows the same order if you haven't bought it yet: pull-up bar, then dip bar, then dumbbells.
Upper day

Same five slots as Beginner; harder variations.

Slot 1

Push-up variation (RPT)

Sets/reps: 3×6–10 RPT; progress at 3×10
Equipment: none; a box or bench to elevate the feet

Full push-ups, then harder versions (feet elevated, more time under tension).

Slot 2

Pull-up / chin-up variation (RPT)

Sets/reps: 3×6–10 RPT, building toward 3×6–8 solid reps
Equipment: pull-up bar

Start every upper day with it. Once you get a first rep, grease the groove. Chin-up or neutral grip is usually easier than a wide pull-up to start ("Get Your First Chin Up / Pull Up", 6:39–7:43).

Slot 3

Dips

Sets/reps: 3×6–10; keep negatives if a full dip is still inconsistent
Equipment: dip bar or 2 sturdy chairs

Slight forward lean, elbows back; shoulders below elbows only if pain-free.

Slot 4

Inverted rows

Sets/reps: 3×6–10; progress the angle toward horizontal, then feet elevated
Equipment: bar; a box to elevate the feet

Same movement as Beginner, harder setup ("Inverted Rows", 0:56; "Variations & Progressions", 5:08–6:07).

Slot 5

Biceps curls (superset with overhead triceps extensions)

Sets/reps: 3×6–10, supersetted
Equipment: a loaded bag, jug, or dumbbells

Same as Beginner.

Lower day

Same five slots as Beginner; harder variations.

Slot 1

Bulgarian split squat (RPT; do it first)

Sets/reps: 3×12; torso upright = more quads, tilted forward = more glutes
Equipment: chair or bench

Full range, back knee almost to the ground (Key Lower Body Exercises PDF; "Bulgarian Split Squats", 1:05–1:23).

Slot 1

Bulgarian split squat, glute bias

Sets/reps: Same 3×12, longer stance and a torso lean
Equipment: chair or bench

Use this setup when you want more glute emphasis, not as a second BSS slot on the same day.

Slot 2

Single-leg Romanian deadlift

Sets/reps: 3×6–10; or two-leg with a loaded bag for more load
Equipment: none, or a bag / dumbbells

Two-leg form remains the standard Romanian deadlift.

Slot 3

Single-leg hip thrust

Sets/reps: 3×6–10
Equipment: none, or a sofa / bench

One leg, hips level ("Hip Thrust Variations", 2:45–3:40).

Slot 4

Reverse hyperextensions (superset with Jefferson curls)

Sets/reps: 3×6–10, supersetted
Equipment: bench or table edge

Back extensions are a related hamstring and glute-chain demo, not a named replacement.

Slot 5

Hanging leg raises

Sets/reps: 3×6–10; add light load to knee raises only after straight-leg raises are solid
Equipment: pull-up bar

Straight legs, a rearward pelvic tilt. The Functional PDF names this slot Weighted Knee Raises Variation.

Warm-up

Same as Beginner: 5 min; at home, 1–2 easy sets of the first exercise with an easier variation. The gym-course warm-up is still the gym version. Optional ATG split squat as a lower-day warm-up.

Gym warm-up (reference only)

Sets/reps: Not the home warm-up
Equipment: bar and weights (gym)

From Aesthetic "How I Warm Up". Wrist work and ramp-up sets with weights. At home, do 1–2 easy sets of the first exercise with an easier variation instead.

ATG split squat (optional lower-day warm-up)

Sets/reps: Easy, not a working set
Equipment: none, or a support to hold

Optional. The course treats this as a useful lower-body warm-up ("ATG Split Squats", 5:34).

Daily movement / steps

Same walking rules as Beginner, further along the same ramp: after the first 2 weeks at 5k, add about 1k every 2 weeks ("2 Best Minimalist Cardio", 0:31). "Tracking is KEY" names consistent 6k, 8k, then 10k. Intermediate is the middle of that ramp — aim for a steady 6k then 8k, not a jump straight to 10k.

Sprints or other hard cardio stay on rest days only, and only if your doctor has cleared them.

Protein & eating

Target: 130 g+ a day, after holding 100 g for a month ("Protein Simplified": Beginner 100 g+, Intermediate 130 g+, Advanced 150 g+).

The staple-meal pattern, fasting window, and the avoid / ask-the-doctor list are the same as Beginner. Still split protein across 2 servings in the 5–8 PM window. Still lean sources unless the doctor has given different saturated-fat guidance.

The samples below are the Beginner windows with portions scaled up. They are estimates, not new course recipes. Weigh the food.

  • A. Adjusted staple, scaled: 5 PM: 300 g raw chicken breast (about 93 g protein), olive-oil spray, 150 g cooked rice, kimchi and seaweed. 7:30 PM: 200 g 0% Greek yogurt (about 20 g) plus a whey shake (about 20 g). Totals: about 133 g protein.
  • B. Fish and tofu, scaled: 5 PM: 180 g raw salmon (about 36 g) and 200 g firm tofu (about 30 g), vegetables and potatoes. 7:30 PM: tinned tuna, 1 can (about 25 g), beans and salad, plus a whey shake (about 20 g) and 150 g 0% yogurt (about 15 g). Totals: about 126 g protein; add a little more yogurt or whey if you land short of 130 g.
  • C. Egg-white egg rice, scaled: 5 PM: 1 whole egg plus 200 g egg whites (about 28 g) and 250 g chicken breast (about 78 g), with rice and soy sauce. 7:30 PM: 200 g 0% yogurt or a whey shake (about 20 g). Totals: about 126 g protein; same short-gap fix as B. Getting 130 g into 3 hours is tighter than 100 g. Keep it to the two servings; don't add a third meal outside the window unless your doctor has changed the window.
Calories

Same method as Beginner (maintenance minus about 400, stay in a 300–500 deficit, never more than 500, don't cut if the target falls below 2,000). Recheck the morning-weight trend when protein and steps go up, because activity can change maintenance slightly. Still no personal number in this package.

From "Maintenance Calories & Calorie Deficit Sweet Spot":

  1. Find your maintenance with a TDEE calculator, using your weight, height, age and activity level. Most men come out at 2,500–2,700. (The course points at the Mayo Clinic calculator.)
  2. Subtract 400. A range of 300–500 is fine; never go more than 500 below. That number is your daily target.
  3. Check it against your morning-weight trend over 2–3 weeks. If your weight isn't falling, lower the target, staying inside the 300–500 range.
  4. If the target comes out below 2,000 kcal, the course says to build muscle at maintenance or a small surplus instead of cutting. Current weight, height and age are not in this package, so there is no personal calorie number here. Example only, not your numbers: maintenance 2,600 gives a target of 2,200. The calorie rule overrides food rules: surplus = gain, deficit = lose ("The First Rule Of Fat Loss"). Pair any cut with the 3×/week lifting so losses are not mostly muscle ("Weight Loss vs Fat Loss").
Sleep

Same as Beginner.

From "Optimizing Sleep":

  • Go to bed at a fixed time (he starts winding down at 10 PM, in bed at 11 PM — use your own fixed clock, not his).
  • Keep your phone out of the bedroom.
  • Get morning sunlight.
  • No intense exercise late at night.
  • Screens off about an hour before bed. Trackers are fine if you don't obsess over them.
Optional extrasOptional

Same list as Beginner. Still optional. Pistol-squat work and Nordics belong here only as extras, not as replacements for BSS or RDL on the Functional days.

These are from "Other Important Exercises / Substitutes" and the 4 key lower-body progressions. They are not required slots on the Functional routine. Use them only as substitutes or add-ons, and don't pile them onto a session until the five main movements are habitual ("Warning!", 1:06–1:44).

ATG split squat

Sets/reps: Optional warm-up or accessory; not a Functional-day slot
Equipment: none, or a support to hold

Knee far over toes, deep range, heel down. Also a lower-body warm-up ("ATG Split Squats", 5:34).

Squat to pistol-squat progression

Sets/reps: Build toward 10 unassisted pistols; stay in a strength range
Equipment: a seat or box for the early steps

Chair/box and assisted pistols first ("Squats To Pistol Squats", 2:07–3:50).

Pistol squat

Sets/reps: Goal from the course: 10 unassisted
Equipment: none

Full single-leg squat, arms forward as a counterbalance. Does not replace BSS on Functional days.

Nordic curls

Sets/reps: Slow lowers; band assistance is fine
Equipment: something heavy to anchor the heels; optional band

Straight hips, lower as slowly as possible. Other Important Exercises; not a Functional-day slot.

Good mornings

Sets/reps: 3×6–10, light load
Equipment: none, or a light bag

Hip hinge, neutral spine.

Back extensions

Sets/reps: 3×6–10
Equipment: bench or table edge

Hinge at the hips, squeeze glutes at the top, don't hyperextend. Related hamstring and glute-chain demo, not the Jefferson curl.

Tibialis raises

Sets/reps: Controlled, slow
Equipment: a wall

Back to the wall, lift the toes.

Patrick step-ups

Sets/reps: Slow, controlled
Equipment: a step or low box

Heel-elevated step-down, knee over toes. Knee-health accessory from the Aesthetic / Ben Patrick work.

Lateral raises

Sets/reps: Higher reps are fine (the aesthetic routine allows up to 20)
Equipment: light dumbbells, a loaded bag, or two books

Lead with elbows, to shoulder height, pinky level with the thumb. Not on the Functional upper day; optional accessory.
