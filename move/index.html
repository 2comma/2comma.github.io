BeginnerYou're here while you work up to a full push-up, a first chin-up or pull-up, and a full Bulgarian split squat; while daily protein is about 100 g; and while daily steps start at 5k and ramp slowly.IntermediateYou're here when full push-ups, chin-ups or pull-ups, and full Bulgarian split squats are your working sets (not just holds and mini ranges); after you've held 100 g of protein a day for a month and are moving to 130 g; and while steps are ramping through 6k and 8k toward 10k.AdvancedYou're here when Intermediate variations are easy at the top of the rep range (about 10–12 good reps), so you move to harder variations: pike or advanced push-ups, weighted or advanced pull-ups, weighted Bulgarian split squats, and L-sits or weighted hanging raises; daily protein is 150 g+; and steps are a consistent 10k.
How the levels work

Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced are skill progressions applied to the same home Upper/Lower routine.

Move up a variation at 3×10 good form. Broader bodyweight rule: 5–12 reps, and once 12 is easy make the exercise harder. Bulgarian split squats: Intermediate is 3×12, then weighted.
