Morning Digest

  1. Fed's Waller says a half-point rate hike is probably needed in December policy Federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller said on Wednesday that a half-percentage-point rate increase is probably needed at the Fed's December 8-9 meeting unless policymakers clearly signal another hike in January. He told CNBC that inflation is spreading and the Fed may need to "shock people."
  2. UK inflation rises to 3.6% in October, a one-year high, as diesel hits a record economy UK consumer price inflation rose to 3.6% in the 12 months to October from 3.5% in September, the Office for National Statistics said on Wednesday. Motor fuels made the largest upward contribution.
  3. World Bank Group to explore USDC stablecoin payments with Citi and Circle crypto The World Bank Group said on Tuesday it will work with Citi and Circle to explore whether regulated US dollar stablecoins can make its fund disbursements faster, cheaper and more transparent. Circle would mint and settle funds in USDC, while Citi would connect the effort to existing banking rails.
  4. DBS lets institutional clients in Asia mint and redeem USDC directly, 24/7 crypto DBS, Southeast Asia's largest bank by assets, said on Wednesday that eligible institutional clients can mint and redeem Circle's USDC stablecoin directly through the bank, with round-the-clock settlement between US dollars and USDC.

Evening Digest

  1. Anthropic prices its IPO at $83 a share, above its marketed range, reports say markets Anthropic priced its initial public offering at $83 per share on Wednesday, above its marketed range of $70 to $78, according to Bloomberg, CNBC, Axios and the Associated Press.
  2. Fed minutes show almost all officials saw further rate hikes as likely appropriate policy Minutes of the Federal Reserve's October 27-28 meeting, released Wednesday, show that almost all participants judged further increases in the federal funds rate would likely be appropriate to return inflation to 2%.
