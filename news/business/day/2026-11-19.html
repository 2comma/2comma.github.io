Morning Digest

  1. Binance wins $6.5 billion worldwide freezing order over October hack crypto The Abu Dhabi Global Market Courts issued a $6.5 billion worldwide freezing order against the unidentified attackers behind Binance's Oct. 29 hack, the exchange and its lawyers at Gibson Dunn said, according to The Block and City AM. The order can be served through the blockchain and comes with a continuously updated list of 26,000 wallet addresses identified as holding stolen assets.
  2. Appeals court rules 2-1 that Maryland can enforce its gambling laws against Kalshi regulation The US Court of Appeals for the Fourth Circuit on Wednesday upheld a lower court's refusal to block Maryland from applying its sports-wagering laws to Kalshi's sports event contracts. It is the third of four federal appeals court decisions to side with state oversight of prediction markets, CoinDesk reported.
  3. US launches Threat Disruption Center to stop scam payments after $20.9 billion in reported losses security The Homeland Security Task Force, co-led by the Homeland Security and Justice departments, launched a Threat Disruption Center to stop victims' money from reaching foreign scam, cybercrime and sextortion networks. It cited $20.9 billion in victim losses reported to the FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center in 2025.
  4. Singapore issues S$100 million of tokenized central bank bills settled in wholesale CBDC banking The Monetary Authority of Singapore said Thursday it issued S$100 million (about $77.5 million) of four-week tokenized MAS Bills on Nov. 6, created directly on its Singapore Dollar Test Network and settled in wholesale central bank digital currency.

Evening Digest

  1. Anthropic closes up 17% in Nasdaq debut after the largest IPO on record markets Anthropic shares ended their first day of trading on Thursday at $97.17, up 17% from the $83 IPO price, after opening at $111.50, according to CBS News and Deadline.
  2. Dow drops 1,101 points as all three major US stock indexes fall about 2% markets The Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 1,101.11 points, or 2.19%, to 49,097.06 on Thursday, while the S&P 500 lost 2.02% and the Nasdaq Composite fell 2.25%, according to AP and Investing.com.
  3. Intuit agrees to sell Mailchimp to Bending Spoons for $3.68 billion earnings Intuit said on Thursday it agreed to sell email-marketing platform Mailchimp to Italy's Bending Spoons for about $3.68 billion, made up of $3.31 billion in cash and $368 million in Bending Spoons stock.
