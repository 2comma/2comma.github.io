US spot bitcoin ETFs post $1.79 billion of net outflows for the week

US spot bitcoin exchange-traded funds had about $1.79 billion of net outflows last week, their fourth straight week of withdrawals, according to SoSoValue data cited by The Block and Cointelegraph. BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust accounted for $1.37 billion of the total.

Mon Nov 23crypto

Strategy buys 457 more bitcoin for $29.6 million, lifting its holdings to 851,305 BTC

Strategy said in a Monday SEC filing that it bought 457 bitcoin between Nov. 16 and Nov. 22 at an average price of $64,898 each. The company now holds 851,305 bitcoin bought for about $64.2 billion, or $75,446 per coin on average.

ECB will accept tokenized securities from EU pilot platforms as collateral from June 2027

The European Central Bank said Monday its Governing Council approved accepting securities issued on distributed ledger platforms licensed under the EU's DLT Pilot Regime as collateral for central bank loans. The ECB plans to put the change into effect in June 2027.

The US Senate on Thursday passed S.4035, the DEATH BETS Act, by voice vote. The bill would bar CFTC-registered exchanges from listing event contracts tied to terrorism, assassination, war or a person's death, and now goes to the House.

Thu Nov 19crypto

Binance wins $6.5 billion worldwide freezing order over October hack

The Abu Dhabi Global Market Courts issued a $6.5 billion worldwide freezing order against the unidentified attackers behind Binance's Oct. 29 hack, the exchange and its lawyers at Gibson Dunn said, according to The Block and City AM.
