Wall Street ends its best week in more than a year as chip stocks rebound

China issues rules for blockchain-based digital identities, effective March 1, 2027

China's Cyberspace Administration and 10 other agencies, including the People's Bank of China, published rules on the interoperability of distributed digital identities, which take effect March 1, 2027.

Fri Nov 27crypto

Russia's VTB opens crypto trading to qualified investors in its app

VTB, one of Russia's largest banks, began letting qualified investors buy and sell bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies in its VTB My Investments app on Friday, a day after the Bank of Russia approved a required knowledge test.

Fri Nov 27crypto

Binance fully lifts its withdrawal suspension four weeks after a $6.3 billion hack

Binance said it has completed its withdrawal recovery work and fully lifted the suspension imposed after the Oct. 29 hack, the largest crypto theft on record. Withdrawals on a small number of networks remain paused for maintenance that the exchange said is unrelated to the attack.

Fri Nov 27economy

China's industrial profits rise 20.1% in October as AI demand lifts electronics makers

Profits at China's large industrial companies rose 20.1% in October from a year earlier, up from 14.4% in September, the National Bureau of Statistics said on Friday. Profits for the first 10 months rose 16.0%.

Fri Nov 27crypto

Brazil's B3 and six major banks plan Open BRL, a real-pegged stablecoin, for early 2027

Brazilian exchange operator B3 said it is working with Bradesco, BTG Pactual, Itaú Unibanco, Safra, Santander and XP to develop Open BRL, a stablecoin pegged to the Brazilian real, along with shared infrastructure for moving money and settling digital-asset trades. Its banking unit, Banco B3, will issue the token.

Fri Nov 27crypto

Ethereum developers set Dec. 15 for the Glamsterdam upgrade on the Hoodi test network

Ethereum's core developers agreed on Thursday to proceed with the Glamsterdam upgrade under its current specification and to activate it on the Hoodi test network on Dec. 15. No date has been set for Ethereum's main network.

Thu Nov 26crypto

Spain gives its anti-money-laundering unit power to freeze crypto accounts without a judge

Spain's financial intelligence unit, Sepblac, will be able to freeze crypto, bank and payment accounts for up to five business days on its own initiative when it suspects money laundering or terrorist financing, under a royal decree-law published this week.

Thu Nov 26courts

New York wins a $1.37 billion judgment against the founders of crypto scheme NovaTech

A New York state court ordered NovaTech founders Cynthia and Eddy Petion to pay $1.37 billion over a cryptocurrency Ponzi scheme that targeted Haitian immigrant communities, Attorney General Letitia James said. The scheme drew in hundreds of thousands of investors worldwide, including about 11,000 New Yorkers.

Thu Nov 26security

Standard Chartered's Zodia Custody says hackers accessed some client contact details

Zodia Custody, the crypto custodian owned by Standard Chartered, said an unauthorized party accessed limited information about some clients and employees, including business contact names and email addresses. The company said its investigation so far has found no unauthorized transactions.

Thu Nov 26policy

Bill requiring the US to hold its bitcoin for at least 20 years is cleared for a House vote

The House Financial Services Committee formally reported a bill that would write a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve into federal law, making it eligible for a vote by the full House. Bitcoin placed in the reserve could not be sold, swapped or otherwise disposed of for at least 20 years.

Thu Nov 26crypto

Switzerland's first spot bitcoin ETF begins trading with 350 BTC

BIT Asset Management's Bitcoin Fund ETF began trading on SIX Swiss Exchange on Thursday under the ticker BBIT, holding 350 bitcoin at launch. The company says it is Switzerland's first spot bitcoin ETF and currently Europe's only one.

Wed Nov 25courts

Nvidia has agreed to pay $290 million in cash to settle a 2018 securities class action alleging it misled investors about how much of its gaming GPU revenue came from cryptocurrency miners, according to a court filing reported by The Block and Decrypt.

Wed Nov 25crypto

Ukraine's parliament passes a law to legalize and tax its crypto market

Ukraine's Verkhovna Rada passed a bill legalizing the country's crypto market and setting tax rules, with 271 lawmakers voting in favor, CoinDesk and The Block reported. The law takes effect in July 2027 and still needs the president's signature.

Wed Nov 25economy

Fed's preferred inflation gauge rises to 3.8% in October

The personal consumption expenditures price index rose 3.8% from a year earlier in October, the Bureau of Economic Analysis said on Wednesday. Core prices, which exclude food and energy, rose 3.3%.

Wed Nov 25policy

US proposes new Section 301 tariffs on 14 trading partners, with a 20% rate on China

The Trump administration on Wednesday proposed new tariffs on 14 economies, including China, the EU, Japan and India, that it accuses of fueling excess industrial capacity. The proposed rate on China is 20% and could rise to 40% if Beijing does not keep to its trade truce with Washington.

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority launched the CMU Digital Asset Platform on Wednesday, a blockchain-based system for issuing, settling and tokenizing debt securities. It supports round-the-clock settlement in several currencies using cash tokens.

Sanofi agrees to buy Insmed for about $41 billion in cash

French drugmaker Sanofi said Tuesday it will pay $175 a share in cash for US biotech company Insmed, valuing it at about $41.0 billion. The price is about 46% above Insmed's closing price on Nov. 18, the day before media reports of a possible deal.

Tue Nov 24regulation

South Korea proposes a crypto law that would let bank-led groups issue won stablecoins and cap exchange stakes at 20%

South Korea's Financial Services Commission (FSC) on Tuesday published a draft digital asset law under which only groups in which banks together own more than 50% could issue won-pegged stablecoins. The draft also caps any major shareholder's stake in a crypto exchange at 20%, or up to 34% with regulator approval.
