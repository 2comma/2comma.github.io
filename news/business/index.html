US spot bitcoin ETFs post $582.2 million of outflows as bitcoin falls below $62,000

Senate passes DEATH BETS Act to ban prediction-market contracts on war and death

The US Senate on Thursday passed S.4035, the DEATH BETS Act, by voice vote. The bill would bar CFTC-registered exchanges from listing event contracts tied to terrorism, assassination, war or a person's death, and now goes to the House.

Crypto payments firm MoonPay said Thursday it has applied to the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency to form MoonPay National Trust Bank, based in Miami Beach, Florida. The proposed bank would offer custody and stablecoin services but would not take deposits or lend.

Thu Nov 19crypto

Binance wins $6.5 billion worldwide freezing order over October hack

The Abu Dhabi Global Market Courts issued a $6.5 billion worldwide freezing order against the unidentified attackers behind Binance's Oct. 29 hack, the exchange and its lawyers at Gibson Dunn said, according to The Block and City AM. The order can be served through the blockchain and comes with a continuously updated list of 26,000 wallet addresses identified as holding stolen assets.

It is the third of four federal appeals court decisions to side with state oversight of prediction markets, CoinDesk reported.
