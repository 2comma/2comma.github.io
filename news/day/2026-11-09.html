Trial finds stopping statins was noninferior to continuing them in older adults without heart disease science Among 1,880 adults aged 70 or older with at least two chronic conditions and no known cardiovascular disease, stopping statins was noninferior to continuing them for major cardiovascular events or death at two years, according to the STREAM trial presented at the American Heart Association's Scientific Sessions and published in the New England Journal of Medicine.
