Asia Digest

  1. Nikkei, KOSPI and Taiex plunge as fears of US curbs on AI data centers hit Asian chip stocks markets Japan's Nikkei 225 fell 5.96% to 57,434.77, South Korea's KOSPI dropped 6.02% to 5,059.69 and Taiwan's Taiex lost 4.51% to 39,701.64 on Friday, as worries that a Democratic-led US Congress could slow AI data-center building hit technology shares, the Japan Times, Yonhap and Focus Taiwan reported.
  2. Malaysia's attorney general halts Najib's move to house arrest after Umno pays his 50 million ringgit fine courts Malaysia's attorney general on Friday temporarily blocked former prime minister Najib Razak's transfer from prison to house arrest, saying more legal clarification was needed, after his party Umno paid the 50 million ringgit fine required under a royal pardon, AP and Malay Mail reported.
  3. Macao court jails former lawmaker Au Kam San for 12.5 years in first national security case courts A Macao court on Friday sentenced former pro-democracy lawmaker Au Kam San, 69, to 12.5 years in prison in the city's first national security case, convicting him of subverting state power, establishing links with outside groups to endanger national security and breach of secrecy, RTHK and AP reported.
  4. Japan's lower house passes bill to cut the consumption tax on food to 1% for two years policy Japan's House of Representatives on Friday passed a bill to cut the consumption tax on food from 8% to 1% for two years from April 2027 and to create income-linked cash payments, sending it to the upper house, where the ruling coalition lacks a majority, NHK, Kyodo and Jiji Press reported.
  5. Singapore and Malaysia to raise cross-border taxi quota to 500 each from Nov. 16 policy Singapore and Malaysia will raise the quota of licensed cross-border taxis from 300 to 500 per country from Nov. 16 and license two more ride-hail operators, the two transport ministries said in a joint statement on Friday.

  2. Godzilla Minus Zero roars to $6.7 million in North American previews film-tv Toho and GKids' Godzilla Minus Zero took in about $6.7 million in North American previews by Friday and was tracking toward roughly a $30 million opening, more than double the debut of 2023's Godzilla Minus One, Variety and Deadline reported.

Lionsgate quarterly revenue jumps 41% on Michael and TV deliveries earnings Lionsgate Studios reported fiscal second-quarter revenue of $668.8 million, up 41% year over year, and narrowed its net loss from continuing operations to $15.5 million, aided by the Michael biopic and higher scripted TV deliveries, the company, Variety and Deadline said.
  4. Sony music segment posts double-digit growth; full-year forecast raised music Sony's music segment reported about 598 billion yen in quarterly revenue and about 123 billion yen in operating income for the three months ended September 2026, and raised its full-year music sales forecast to 2.30 trillion yen, Billboard and Music Business Worldwide reported from Sony results.

CMS proposes CARE rule to cut hospital and nursing-home administrative burdens policy The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services on Thursday issued the Cutting Administrative Requirements for Excellence in Patient Care (CARE) proposed rule, estimating it would save $320 million a year and return more than 3.26 million staff hours to providers.
