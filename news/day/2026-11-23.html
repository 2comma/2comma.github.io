BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust accounted for $1.37 billion of the total.
  3. Strategy buys 457 more bitcoin for $29.6 million, lifting its holdings to 851,305 BTC crypto Strategy said in a Monday SEC filing that it bought 457 bitcoin between Nov. 16 and Nov. 22 at an average price of $64,898 each. The company now holds 851,305 bitcoin bought for about $64.2 billion, or $75,446 per coin on average.

ECB will accept tokenized securities from EU pilot platforms as collateral from June 2027 banking The European Central Bank said Monday its Governing Council approved accepting securities issued on distributed ledger platforms licensed under the EU's DLT Pilot Regime as collateral for central bank loans. The ECB plans to put the change into effect in June 2027.
