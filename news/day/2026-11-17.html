Chairman Michael Selig said the approvals show there is a regulated pathway for market infrastructure built on blockchain technology.
  4. Google says hackers used AI to fully automate exploits for the first time ai Google Threat Intelligence Group said in a report released on November 16 that it had, for the first time, identified threat actors using large language model frameworks to fully automate vulnerability discovery and exploitation without a human in the loop. It found the multi-agent system, called exploit_agent, on an exposed staging server.
