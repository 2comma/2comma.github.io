Brazil's B3 and six major banks plan Open BRL, a real-pegged stablecoin, for early 2027 crypto Brazilian exchange operator B3 said it is working with Bradesco, BTG Pactual, Itaú Unibanco, Safra, Santander and XP to develop Open BRL, a stablecoin pegged to the Brazilian real, along with shared infrastructure for moving money and settling digital-asset trades.
