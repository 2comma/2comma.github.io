South Africa's ANC on course for sharp drop in municipal election vote share as counting continues elections With 71% of votes counted, South Africa's African National Congress was on 32% of the municipal election vote against 25% for the Democratic Alliance, according to Reuters; the ANC would be down about 14 percentage points from 2021 if the figures hold.
