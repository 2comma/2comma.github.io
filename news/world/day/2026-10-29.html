World Digest

Spain train crash death toll reaches 68 as minister blames 'inexplicable' control-centre order disaster At least 68 people were killed when a high-speed Renfe train struck a stationary Iryo train near Urda in central Spain on Wednesday, and Transport Minister Óscar Puente said a control-centre decision to let the train resume at normal speed is at the centre of the investigation, according to the BBC, The Guardian and Euronews.
  2. Moscow mayor says 613 drones flew toward the Russian capital overnight conflict Moscow Mayor Sergei Sobyanin said 613 drones flew toward the Moscow region between Wednesday evening and Thursday morning, with 231 destroyed on approach to the city and most of the rest neutralized farther out, according to Reuters, via Al Jazeera, and Meduza.
  3. Flávio Bolsonaro lands in Miami and says he did not flee Brazil elections Brazil's defeated presidential candidate Flávio Bolsonaro arrived in the Miami area on Wednesday after Brazil cancelled his passports in a criminal investigation, and said in a statement that he "did not flee Brazil," according to the BBC and The Guardian.
  4. El Salvador sends more than 2,000 troops to Haiti to find four missing soldiers as Haiti delays its elections conflict El Salvador's foreign ministry said Wednesday it is deploying more than 2,000 soldiers to Haiti to search for four Salvadoran air force technicians missing since an Oct. 18 ambush and to help fight gangs, according to the BBC and Al Jazeera.
  5. Ethiopia says it has received Russian Su-35 fighter jets as fighting continues in Tigray diplomacy Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed said Wednesday that his country has received Su-35 fighter jets from Russia and plans to acquire four S-400 air defence systems, as government forces fight in the northern Tigray region, according to the BBC and Al Jazeera.
