Asia Digest

  1. Taiex jumps 4.94% in its second-largest daily point gain after Taiwan authorizes stabilization fund markets Taiwan's Taiex rose 1,960.06 points, or 4.94%, to 41,661.70 on Monday, its second-largest single-day point gain, after the National Financial Stabilization Fund's management committee authorized market intervention on Friday, Focus Taiwan reported.
  2. South Korea fines Baemin and Coupang a combined 631.9 billion won over price-matching demands on restaurants regulation South Korea's Fair Trade Commission said Monday it will impose provisional fines of 331.1 billion won on Woowa Brothers, operator of delivery app Baemin, and 300.8 billion won on Coupang, which runs Coupang Eats, for requiring restaurants to offer terms no less favorable than on rival apps, Yonhap and the Korea Herald reported.
  3. Mitsui Chemicals to buy chip materials maker JSR for 1.84 trillion yen with Development Bank of Japan business Mitsui Chemicals said Monday it will acquire semiconductor materials maker JSR from state-backed Japan Investment Corp. (JIC) for 1.84 trillion yen ($11.61 billion), with the Development Bank of Japan (DBJ) investing 200 billion yen, according to a company filing and Reuters.
  4. Japan and Vietnam sign 10 deals on energy, technology and security as To Lam visits Tokyo diplomacy Japan and Vietnam announced 10 agreements and pledged to raise bilateral trade to as much as $100 billion by 2030 as Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi met Vietnamese President and Communist Party General Secretary To Lam in Tokyo on Monday, Bloomberg reported in the Japan Times.
  5. Japanese retailer Nitori says up to 37.62 million customer records may have leaked after unauthorized access security Japanese home furnishings retailer Nitori Holdings said Monday that up to 37.62 million customer records, including email and home addresses, may have leaked through unauthorized access, though login passwords and credit card details were not compromised, Kyodo and Nikkei reported.
