South Korea's exports jump 93.7% to a record $115.3 billion in October on chip demand economy South Korea's exports rose 93.7% from a year earlier to a record $115.3 billion in October, driven by a 259% jump in semiconductor shipments, the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Resources said on Sunday.

Japan launches a national agency to lead disaster preparedness and response disaster Japan's government on Monday launched the National Disaster Management Agency, a new body headed by Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi to coordinate the country's response to disasters, according to AP, Kyodo News and NHK.

Taiwan says China's handling of its APEC summit invitation broke with established practice diplomacy Taiwan's Foreign Minister Lin Chia-lung on Monday called on China, this year's APEC host, to follow established practice in formally inviting Taiwan to the Nov. 18-19 leaders' summit in Shenzhen, according to Focus Taiwan and the Taipei Times.
  4. Johor-Singapore economic zone has drawn 88.83 billion ringgit in approved investments, ministers say economy The Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone recorded 88.83 billion ringgit (S$28.1 billion) in approved investments from January 2025 to June 2026, Malaysian and Singaporean ministers said in a joint statement on Monday, according to CNA and The Business Times.
  5. Japan urges US to enforce troop discipline after another Marine is arrested in Okinawa diplomacy Japan asked the United States on Monday to strictly enforce discipline among its troops after a US Marine was arrested in Okinawa on suspicion of drink-driving during a curfew, Defense Minister Shinjiro Koizumi and Chief Cabinet Secretary Minoru Kihara said, according to CNA and Kyodo News.
