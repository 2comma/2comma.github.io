  1. BN-PN alliance wins 24 of 28 seats in Malaysia's Melaka election, a setback for Anwar elections The Barisan Nasional (BN) and Perikatan Nasional (PN) alliance won 24 of the 28 seats in Malaysia's Melaka state election on Saturday, a two-thirds majority, CNA and The Straits Times reported.

ASEAN signs region-wide digital economy agreement, aiming to start in early 2027 economy ASEAN member states signed the Digital Economy Framework Agreement (DEFA) in Manila on Saturday, a legally binding pact on digital trade, data flows, payments and cybersecurity, CNA and The Straits Times reported.

About 140,000 migrant worker records exposed in Singapore's Fullerton Health breach security About 140,000 work pass holder records were compromised in a breach of a Fullerton Health portal used for Singapore's Migrant Worker Primary Care Plan, the healthcare provider said Saturday, according to CNA and The Straits Times.
  4. Hong Kong Disneyland halts online ticket sales after about 17,000 QR codes stolen security Hong Kong Disneyland suspended online ticket sales and hotel bookings after about 17,000 e-ticket QR codes were stolen from its online ticketing system, the South China Morning Post and The Standard reported.
  5. Myanmar military airstrike on Rakhine displacement camp kills 12, witnesses say conflict A Myanmar military airstrike on a camp for people displaced by the civil war killed 12 people in Rakhine state on Friday evening, witnesses and a rescue worker said, according to AFP and Al Jazeera.
