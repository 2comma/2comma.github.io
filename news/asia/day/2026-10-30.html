Asia Digest

  1. Bank of Japan holds rate at 1.25%, says underlying inflation is generally at its 2% target economy The Bank of Japan kept its policy rate at around 1.25% on Friday and said underlying inflation is now generally consistent with its 2% target, the first time it has made that assessment since adopting the goal in 2013, according to Kyodo News, NHK and CNBC.
  2. Seoul and Taipei stocks tumble as AI spending worries hit chipmakers markets South Korea's KOSPI fell 3.48% to 5,730.74 on Friday and Taiwan's Taiex dropped 2.49% to 45,211.83, as chip stocks slid after a tech sell-off on Wall Street, according to Yonhap, Edaily and Focus Taiwan.
  3. Apple to cut Taiwan App Store commission to 26% after Fair Trade Commission probe regulation Apple will lower its standard App Store commission in Taiwan to 26% from 30% starting Oct. 31, a commitment Taiwan's Fair Trade Commission said on Friday it had accepted to address competition concerns, according to Apple and the commission.
  4. Toyota and Waymo to start testing driverless bZ4X robotaxis in Japan in 2027 ai-tech Toyota and Waymo said on Friday they will begin testing and validating a fleet of autonomous Toyota bZ4X vehicles in Japan in 2027, with deployment to the United Kingdom and other Waymo markets planned in later years, according to the companies and Kyodo News.
  5. Taiwan's legislature allows death penalty for repeat drug-impaired drivers who kill policy Taiwan's Legislative Yuan on Friday passed Criminal Code amendments that raise penalties for drug-impaired driving and allow the death penalty for repeat offenders who cause a death within 10 years, according to Focus Taiwan and Taiwan News.
