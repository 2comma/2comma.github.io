Hong Kong leader apologises over Wang Fuk Court fire as inquiry finds four government bodies partly responsible public-safety Hong Kong Chief Executive John Lee apologised on Thursday for the November 2025 fire at Wang Fuk Court in Tai Po that killed 168 people, as a judge-led inquiry report found four government bodies partly responsible and Housing Secretary Winnie Ho resigned, according to Reuters and AFP.
  2. North Korea fires about a dozen short-range ballistic missiles toward the East Sea conflict North Korea fired about a dozen short-range ballistic missiles from the Wonsan area toward the East Sea on Thursday, South Korea's Joint Chiefs of Staff said, and Seoul's presidential office urged Pyongyang to stop what it called provocations, according to Yonhap and The Korea Herald.
  3. Samsung Electronics posts record 107.4 trillion won quarterly operating profit on AI memory demand earnings Samsung Electronics said its third-quarter operating profit reached a record 107.4 trillion won on revenue of 195.3 trillion won, driven by its memory chip business, though its shares closed 4.13% lower on Thursday, according to the company and CNBC.
  4. Special counsel again seeks death penalty for South Korea's former President Yoon on appeal courts A special counsel team asked the Seoul High Court on Thursday to sentence former President Yoon Suk Yeol to death on insurrection charges over his December 2024 martial law declaration, after a lower court gave him life imprisonment in February, according to Yonhap and SBS.
  5. Japan's ruling coalition and DPP agree to cut 31 Lower House seats politics Japan's Liberal Democratic Party, its coalition partner the Japan Innovation Party and the opposition Democratic Party for the People agreed on Thursday to cut 31 proportional representation seats from the 465-seat House of Representatives, according to Kyodo News and The Japan Times.
