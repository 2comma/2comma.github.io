NATO sends an AWACS surveillance plane to Poland after Russian missiles land there

Polish Deputy Defense Minister Pawel Zalewski announced the deployment after a NATO meeting in Brussels, Reuters reported. A NATO spokesperson said Supreme Allied Commander Europe Gen. Alexus Grynkewich approved the move and is examining whether to send more assets, according to Reuters and Anadolu Agency.

Polish prosecutors said the three missiles landed on Friday, Reuters reported. No operating base or duration for the aircraft was announced.

Why it matters: The deployment is part of NATO's response to the incident on its eastern flank, which has raised fears that Russia's war in Ukraine could spill into alliance territory, Reuters reported.
