Chainflip says hack losses reach about $12 million after 122.86 BTC was also taken

Chainflip offered the attacker a 10% bounty if the other 90% is returned by Monday at 1:38 p.m. ET, and said it had found and fixed the vulnerability, according to The Block. The Ethereum proceeds had already been sent to Tornado Cash, while the stolen bitcoin had not moved, onchain data showed.

Why it matters: The figure is more than double the $4.86 million first reported, and the attack appears to have involved a compromise of the protocol's shared signing key, The Block reported.
