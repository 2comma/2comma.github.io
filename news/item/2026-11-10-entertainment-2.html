Ella Langley's 'Choosin' Texas' extends its record Hot 100 run to 27 weeks as 'Thriller' climbs to No. 2

"Thriller" reached its new high 42 years and nine months after it first entered the chart, according to Billboard. Jackson's Thriller album also rose to No. 2 on the Billboard 200, putting him in the top two of both charts for the first time since January 1992, Billboard said.

Olivia Rodrigo's "you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love" returned to No. 1 on the Billboard 200 for a fourth week with 57,000 equivalent album units, about 2,000 more than Thriller, Ratings Game Music reported.

Why it matters: "Choosin' Texas" now has five more weeks at No. 1 than the next-longest run, Mariah Carey's "All I Want for Christmas Is You," at 22 weeks, according to Billboard.
