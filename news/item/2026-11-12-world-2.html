Thursday · Nov 12, 2026 · World Digest · conflict

Russia strikes Kyiv with missiles and drones hours after Abu Dhabi talks end; one killed in apartment hit

Ukraine's Air Force said Russia launched ballistic and Zircon missiles and 123 long-range attack drones at Ukraine overnight, and that air defenses had intercepted 89 drones by about 8 a.m. local time, The Kyiv Independent reported. National police said damage was reported in eight regions, mainly to energy and transport infrastructure.

Russia's military said it had carried out a massive overnight strike on military factories across Ukraine, Reuters reported. Kyiv prosecutors said the strike on the apartment building in the Solomianskyi district started a fire on the third to sixth floors, according to Interfax-Ukraine.

Why it matters: The attack followed the end of US-brokered talks in Abu Dhabi that concluded without a meaningful breakthrough, The Kyiv Independent reported.
