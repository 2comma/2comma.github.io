Saturday · Nov 21, 2026 · Weekend Digest · conflict

Drones hit Russia-bound cargo ship in Gulf of Finland; Finland says Ukraine likely behind it

"War is spilling over into our immediate vicinity," Finnish Prime Minister Petteri Orpo said, according to the BBC. The Finnish Border Guard said the crew was evacuated to another Russian vessel and no environmental damage was detected, and the BBC reported the ship was sanctioned by the US in 2022. France 24 also reported the drone attack set the ship ablaze.

Why it matters: The strike brings the Russia-Ukraine war into waters next to NATO member Finland, where officials imposed temporary aviation and maritime restrictions.
