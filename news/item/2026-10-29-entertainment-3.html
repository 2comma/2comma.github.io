Thursday · Oct 29, 2026 · Entertainment Digest · film-tv

Netflix develops live-action 'PUBG' series with Donald Glover directing and starring

Glover is writing and executive producing with his brother Stephen Glover, according to Deadline. Executive producers also include Michael Schaefer of Neon TV, Fam Udeorji of Gilga and Krafton's entertainment unit LV3H. No release date has been announced.

The project joins other video game adaptations in development at Netflix, including a live-action "Assassin's Creed" series and a live-action "Gears of War" film, Deadline reported. The game, originally titled "PlayerUnknown's Battlegrounds," debuted in 2017.

Why it matters: The deal extends a push by streamers to adapt video game franchises, and Krafton has been working since 2020 to expand PUBG into film and TV, according to Deadline.
