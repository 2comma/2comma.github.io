Virginia and Oregon sue SpaceX over sexual deepfakes made with Grok

The attorneys general filed separate consumer-protection complaints in state courts on Sunday and Monday against SpaceX, which owns X and xAI, according to CNBC and the Virginia attorney general's office. Both states seek injunctions, civil penalties and disgorgement, and Virginia also seeks restitution for consumers, CNBC reported.

In a statement to CNBC, SpaceX's AI unit said it takes action against accounts that try to break its safeguards and notifies law enforcement. AP also reported the suits.

Why it matters: The suits add state-level legal pressure on Grok, which already faces probes in the UK, Spain, Ireland, France and California, CNBC reported.
