Schumer says Democrats will block a right-wing Supreme Court nominee

In an Associated Press interview cited by The Guardian, Schumer said Democrats would use their incoming majority to reject nominees they oppose once the new Congress is seated in January. Democrats hold at least 54 Senate seats, with Alaska and Mississippi still uncalled, the outlets said.

NBC News and AP said the Democratic Senate could thwart Supreme Court and other judicial appointments for the remainder of Trump's term if vacancies arise. Justice Samuel Alito told Fox News he plans to stay on the court for the remainder of the term, The Guardian reported, after speculation about possible retirements under a Republican Senate.

Why it matters: Control of the Senate confirmation process shifts to Democrats, changing the outlook for any late-term Supreme Court or Cabinet nominations.
