Thursday · Nov 5, 2026 · World Digest · elections

Israel's president accepts election results and opens talks on who will form the next government

Herzog received the official results from the Central Elections Committee and said he adopted them in accordance with the law, according to the text of his remarks published by The Times of Israel. "The elections are over. Now is the time to face reality," he said, as quoted by Al Jazeera.

AP reported that the move dealt a blow to Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's efforts to discredit the vote, in which he and his allies lost their majority in the 120-seat Knesset. Al Jazeera reported that neither Netanyahu's coalition, with 53 seats, nor the bloc of opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot, with 48, reached the 61 needed to govern. Consultations with parties continued on Thursday.

Why it matters: After the consultations, Herzog picks the lawmaker he sees as best placed to build a coalition. That nominee then has 28 days to form a government, with a possible 14-day extension, according to Al Jazeera, and if no one succeeds Israel would face another election.
