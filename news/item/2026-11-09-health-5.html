Pennsylvania tops 1,500 measles cases as PAHO panel meets this week on U.S. elimination status

The state health department reported 73 new cases in the past seven days, including a rare breakthrough case in a vaccinated person, and has confirmed six measles-associated deaths while investigating a seventh, the Inquirer reported. Nationally, 5,182 cases had been reported this year as of last Thursday, according to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention.

PAHO said its regional re-verification commission will review evidence including vaccination coverage, surveillance and laboratory data, and that endemic transmission is considered re-established when the same strain circulates uninterrupted for 12 months or longer. The commission's conclusions go to the PAHO director.

Why it matters: The United States has held measles elimination status since 2000, the Inquirer noted. PAHO said Canada is currently the only country in the Americas classified as endemic for measles.
