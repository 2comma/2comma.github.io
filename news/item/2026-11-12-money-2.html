Freddie Mac: 30-year mortgage rate falls to 7.11%, a fourth straight weekly decline

The 15-year fixed rate averaged 6.45%, down from 6.57%, Freddie Mac said. A year ago, the 30-year rate averaged 6.24% and the 15-year rate 5.49%.

Mortgage Professional America reported that the 10-year Treasury yield rose on Thursday as oil prices climbed, after hotter-than-expected October inflation data raised expectations of a Federal Reserve rate increase in December. Freddie Mac's weekly survey averages rates on applications from the prior Thursday through Wednesday.

Why it matters: Rates remain roughly 90 basis points above year-earlier levels, according to Mortgage Professional America.
