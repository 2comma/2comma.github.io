Second batch of three F-16V Block 70 jets arrives in Taiwan

The jets, numbered 6729, 6731 and 6732, arrived at about 11 a.m. after flying from Lockheed Martin's factory in South Carolina via Hawaii, the reports said. The first two jets were delivered on Oct. 2. The air force said it would carry out familiarization training to build the aircraft's initial operational capability.

The US approved the sale of 66 F-16Vs to Taiwan in 2019 for about US$8 billion; deliveries were expected to start in 2023 but were delayed by technical issues, the Taipei Times reported.

Why it matters: The jets are part of a 66-aircraft, roughly US$8 billion order that has fallen years behind its original schedule.
