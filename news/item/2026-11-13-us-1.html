Friday · Nov 13, 2026 · US Digest · courts

Appeals court says US wrongly withheld $73.5 million in highway funds from New York over immigrant truck licenses

The department had said New York wrongly issued licenses that ran past the expiration of drivers' lawful-presence documents. Judge Allison Nathan wrote that the regulations in effect at the time contained no such expiration-matching requirement, Courthouse News reported. Judge Matthew Schwartz agreed with the judgment but wrote separately, the outlet said.

A Transportation Department spokesperson said in a statement that the administration would keep using every resource to keep unqualified drivers off the roads, Courthouse News reported. The department had also threatened to withhold another $147 million in future years.

Why it matters: New York officials argued Congress allocated the money for road maintenance, safety improvements and infrastructure projects, Courthouse News reported.
