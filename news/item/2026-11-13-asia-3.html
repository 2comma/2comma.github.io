Friday · Nov 13, 2026 · Asia Digest · singapore

Singapore's cyber agency says reported incidents have tripled since September

The agency said the incidents do not appear to be part of coordinated campaigns targeting Singapore but reflect a broader global trend of opportunistic attacks, some involving compromised third-party suppliers, The Straits Times and CNA reported. It urged organisations to secure internet-facing systems, patch known vulnerabilities, use multi-factor authentication and monitor for suspicious activity.

At least 12 cyber incidents involving Singapore organisations or their service providers were reported between Oct. 24 and Nov. 13, six of them involving healthcare and community care organisations, The Straits Times reported.

Why it matters: The agency warned that attackers' growing use of AI could speed up the discovery and exploitation of vulnerabilities.
