Court filings show U.S. held talks with Iran on deporting Iranians during the war

In an Oct. 15 hearing transcript cited by ABC News, a Justice Department attorney told U.S. District Judge Ana Reyes that planned removals of Iranian nationals to Iran via third countries were under way, after the government had previously said there were none. Reyes ordered 24-hour notice of any finalized deportation plan and five days' written notice before placing someone on a transport for removal, warning of contempt proceedings for violations, ABC News reported.

AP reported the unsealed filings show the administration said as recently as mid-October that it planned to deport Iranians through an unnamed third country. The disclosures came in a lawsuit by the Iranian American Legal Defense Fund alleging the government shared confidential information about Iranian asylum seekers with Iran. A Department of Homeland Security spokesperson did not immediately comment to ABC News.

Why it matters: The filings put wartime deportation logistics before a federal judge who has barred removals without advance notice.
