North Atlantic right whale population falls to an estimated 382

The population remains about 20% below its 2011 peak of 483, and there are an estimated 73 reproductive females, the aquarium said. AP reported that the drop alarmed scientists.

Why it matters: The North Atlantic right whale is critically endangered, and the new count shows the population is still shrinking, the aquarium said.
