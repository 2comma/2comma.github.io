Friday · Nov 27, 2026 · Evening Digest · markets

Wall Street ends its best week in more than a year as chip stocks rebound

The Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 591.99 points, or 1.18%, to 50,774.94, Reuters reported. Chipmakers led the gains: Micron rose 7.6%, Marvell 7.3% and a broad semiconductor index 4.7%, while Nvidia ended 0.8% higher, according to Reuters. The Associated Press said the rally gave Wall Street its best week in more than a year after several days of sharp swings.

All 11 S&P 500 sectors finished higher, with consumer discretionary in the lead, Kiplinger reported.

Why it matters: The rebound follows a steep selloff in AI-related stocks this month, driven by worries over AI spending, borrowing and returns, Reuters reported.
