30-year mortgage rate falls to 7.18%, a third straight weekly drop

The average 30-year fixed mortgage rate fell to 7.18% this week from 7.24%, its third consecutive weekly decline, Freddie Mac said Thursday. The average 15-year fixed rate eased to 6.57% from 6.60%. A year ago, the two rates averaged 6.22% and 5.50%. Freddie Mac chief economist Sam Khater said rates continued their downward trajectory, improving the environment for prospective homebuyers against a backdrop of strong economic growth. Freddie Mac's weekly survey covers conventional, conforming home purchase loans for borrowers who put 20% down and have excellent credit.

Why it matters: Lower rates trim monthly payments for new buyers, but borrowing costs remain nearly a percentage point above year-ago levels. The survey reflects well-qualified borrowers, so many people will still be quoted higher rates.
