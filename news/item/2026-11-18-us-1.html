Man in Iran-backed plot to assassinate US officials sentenced to more than 27 years

A jury convicted Merchant in March of attempting to commit an act of terrorism transcending national boundaries and murder for hire, according to the Justice Department and the BBC. The hitmen he tried to hire were undercover FBI agents, and prosecutors said he paid them $5,000 as an initial payment, the BBC reported.

Potential targets included President Donald Trump, former President Joe Biden and former UN ambassador Nikki Haley, the BBC reported. Merchant said in court that he acted under the direction of the Revolutionary Guard to avenge the 2020 killing of Qasem Soleimani.

Why it matters: The case is one of the most serious prosecutions over Iran-linked plots against US officials.
