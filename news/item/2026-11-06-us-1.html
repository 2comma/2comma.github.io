Friday · Nov 6, 2026 · US Digest · courts

Judge issues 14-day block on construction of Trump's Washington arch

USA Today said the order, sought by the District of Columbia and Virginia, blocks construction, excavation, demolition, land-disturbing work, utility installation, roadway closures and related activity at Memorial Circle on Columbus Island while the court considers the case. Defendants must update the court by Nov. 9 on notices to employees and contractors, USA Today reported.

AP reported the planning commission's approval had cleared a path toward early excavation; the Justice Department had previously said initial foundation work could begin within days. Local officials argue Congress never authorized the roughly 250-foot monument; the administration contends older bridge legislation provides authority, the outlets said. The Guardian said the halt came a day after commission approval.

Why it matters: A federal judge has paused groundbreaking on a high-profile White House-backed monument project amid a fight over legal authority and local impacts.
