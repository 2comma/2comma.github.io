Monday · Nov 9, 2026 · Asia Digest · diplomacy

Japan and Vietnam sign 10 deals on energy, technology and security as To Lam visits Tokyo

Takaichi said the two sides discussed gas-fired and LNG power projects involving Japanese companies as well as nuclear energy cooperation, the Japan Times reported. Nikkei Asia said the countries agreed to cooperate on introducing small modular reactors in Vietnam as early as 2040.

It is the first visit to Japan by a sitting Vietnamese Communist Party chief in 11 years, Nikkei Asia and Bloomberg reported. Kyodo said Tokyo is seeking stronger ties with regional partners with an eye on economic pressure from China.

Why it matters: Nikkei Asia said the agreements, which also cover artificial intelligence, come as Vietnam's power needs surge.
