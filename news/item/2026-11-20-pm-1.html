Friday · Nov 20, 2026 · Evening Digest · markets

S&P 500 and Dow enter correction as the Nasdaq turns negative for the year

The S&P 500 closed at 6,910.39, and the Dow fell 949.73 points, or 1.93%, to 48,147.33, CNBC reported. The Nasdaq Composite dropped 5.28% to 22,617.73 and is now down 2.7% for the year, according to CNBC. Reuters reported that the S&P 500 posted its seventh straight decline, its longest losing streak since February 2020.

CNBC said Nvidia fell about 14% and Anthropic lost 8.5% in its second day of trading. For the week, the S&P 500 fell 8.6% and the Nasdaq about 10%, its biggest weekly drop in more than six years, CNBC reported.

Why it matters: It is the S&P 500's first correction since 2025, according to AP, and it extends a sell-off in the AI stocks that had led this year's rally, Reuters reported.
