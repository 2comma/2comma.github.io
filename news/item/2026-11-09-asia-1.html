Monday · Nov 9, 2026 · Asia Digest · markets

Taiex jumps 4.94% in its second-largest daily point gain after Taiwan authorizes stabilization fund

The rebound ended a seven-session decline that had pushed the technology-heavy market into bear territory, Bloomberg reported. Focus Taiwan said the index had fallen about 20.55% from its Oct. 6 intraday high of 49,968.92 before Monday's rally, and that TSMC, which accounts for more than 40% of the Taiex's market value, gained 4.68% to NT$2,235.

Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission also cut the cap on intraday short-sale orders for borrowed securities to 3% of a stock's average daily volume over the prior 30 days, from 30%, and raised the minimum margin requirement for short selling to 130% from 90%, Bloomberg reported.

Why it matters: Focus Taiwan said it was the 10th time the fund, set up in 2000 with NT$500 billion, has authorized intervention to curb market volatility.
