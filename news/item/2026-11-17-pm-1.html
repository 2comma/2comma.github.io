Nvidia posts record $115.8 billion quarter as board says it will not reinstate Jensen Huang

Data center revenue rose 110% to $107.6 billion, Nvidia said in its earnings release. The Verge reported that net profit reached a record $65.3 billion, and Decrypt reported that the company forecast about $130 billion in fourth-quarter revenue.

Opening the earnings call, board chair Harvey Jones said the board "does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO" and would take no questions on his resignation or the ongoing investigation, The Verge and TechCrunch reported. Huang had said on X earlier on Tuesday that he would be "honored" to return if the board asked. Interim CEO Colette Kress remains in charge while the board searches internal and external candidates, according to TechCrunch.

Why it matters: It was Nvidia's first report since Huang's exit, and the board's statement answers the open question of whether he could come back.
