Bitcoin falls below $59,000 as the sell-off spreads from AI stocks to crypto

Cointelegraph and Decrypt reported that bitcoin fell below $60,000 earlier in the day for the first time since July. CoinDesk said ether fell 7% and that about $1.5 billion of leveraged crypto positions were liquidated over 24 hours, citing CoinGlass.

Bitcoin miners tied to AI data centers fell further, with Hut 8 down nearly 15%, according to CoinDesk.

Why it matters: The drop erases bitcoin's late-summer rebound to $87,000, CoinDesk said, and leaves it close to a new low for the year.
