South Africa's ANC stays largest party with 30.76% in final local election results

The commission said the uMkhonto weSizwe Party took 9.76%, the Economic Freedom Fighters 6.22% and ActionSA 5.67%, SAnews and IOL reported. Turnout was 52%, up from 45.9% in 2021, with about 14.9 million of 29.2 million registered voters casting ballots, according to the commission.

No party won a majority of seats in 127 of the 213 municipalities, which are now hung councils, the commission said. The ANC won outright control of 53 municipalities and the DA of 12, according to SAnews.

Why it matters: Reuters said the result is a further setback for the ANC, whose local vote share has fallen over 15 years largely because of failures in basic services such as water and electricity. IOL reported that the fragmented results put coalition government in the spotlight.
