Friday · Nov 13, 2026 · Politics Digest · policy

Bessent says other agencies backed restricting Megaspeed for months but Commerce has not acted

"You will have to ask them about their internal processes," Bessent told CNBC, referring to Commerce, which is led by Howard Lutnick. He said "any leakage is very frustrating" when President Donald Trump has made clear China should not receive Nvidia chips more advanced than the H100 model.

A Commerce spokeswoman had told CNBC earlier in the week that a reported Situation Room exchange between Bessent and Lutnick did not happen. Bessent said he does not repeat Situation Room conversations, CNBC reported.

Why it matters: The comments point to a split inside the administration over chip export controls after Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia CEO amid scrutiny of his ties to a Megaspeed executive.
