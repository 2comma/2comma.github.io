Treasury's FinCEN discloses unauthorized access to some of its systems

FinCEN collects the suspicious activity reports and other filings that banks must submit under the Bank Secrecy Act. Its filing website remains up, but some services are unavailable, and a FinCEN order gives affected filers with reports due between Nov. 18 and Dec. 29 until Dec. 30 to submit them. The Verge and ABC News reported the disclosure, which came hours after reports of a separate breach at US intelligence agencies; it is not clear whether the incidents are related.

Why it matters: FinCEN holds sensitive financial records used in criminal, tax and national security investigations, and the outage disrupts a reporting system that the financial sector is required by law to use.
