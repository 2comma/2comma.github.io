Thursday · Nov 5, 2026 · Asia Digest · markets

Seoul stocks plunge 5.28% and Taipei drops 3.2% as AI worries hit chipmakers after US midterms

The Korea Exchange triggered a sell-side "sidecar" curb that paused program trading after the KOSPI fell more than 5% during the session, Yonhap and Reuters reported. Reuters said chipmakers tracked overnight losses in U.S. tech stocks, and Yonhap tied the selloff to concerns that a Democratic-controlled Congress could tighten rules on AI data centers.

In Taipei, the Taiex lost 1,374.19 points and closed at its lowest level since July 31, Focus Taiwan reported, citing the Taiwan Stock Exchange.

Why it matters: Chipmakers are among the largest listed companies in both markets, so shifts in expectations for AI spending move the two indexes sharply.
