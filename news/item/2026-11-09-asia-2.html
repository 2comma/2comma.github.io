South Korea fines Baemin and Coupang a combined 631.9 billion won over price-matching demands on restaurants

The commission said the so-called most-favored-nation requirements weakened competition, pushed up food prices and made it harder for new players to enter the market, Yonhap reported. Restaurants that refused risked exclusion from Coupang's Wow membership program or the Baemin Club, and the two platforms held 92.89% of South Korea's food delivery market last year, the Korea Herald reported.

Baemin was also sanctioned for effectively pushing restaurants onto its own delivery service and for misleading delivery-time displays, the Herald Business reported. The commission ordered a re-examination of a separate allegation that Coupang tied delivery benefits to its Wow membership, Yonhap reported.

Why it matters: The Korea Herald said the combined penalty is the largest ever imposed on food delivery app operators in South Korea.
