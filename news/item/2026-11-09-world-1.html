Trump says US land blockade of Iran is in effect as Iran reports tankers hit mines near Hormuz

US Central Command said its commander, Adm. Brad Cooper, set up the task force on November 7 as a staff of about 20 civilian and military personnel in Tampa, Florida, to coordinate with other agencies against the movement of advanced weapons, dual-use technology, sanctioned materials and illegal revenue. Trump told CNN that fresh US strikes on Iran were "highly unlikely" to be needed if talks progress, Reuters and Al Jazeera reported. Secretary of State Marco Rubio said he did not believe a breakthrough was imminent, calling some Iranian demands unrealistic, according to Reuters.

Iran's Revolutionary Guards navy said the two tankers were using an "unauthorized route" through the strait, and the US military said its air force disabled a commercial vessel trying to get through the US naval blockade in the Gulf of Oman on Saturday, Reuters reported. Reuters said it could not confirm any of the reported strikes.

Why it matters: Reuters said attacks on shipping by both sides are restricting energy exports through the Strait of Hormuz. CENTCOM said its naval blockade has kept Iran from exporting more than $25 billion in crude oil in recent months.
