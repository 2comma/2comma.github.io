Google releases Gemini 4 Neon AI model at $3 per million input tokens

The model is rolling out in the paid Gemini API, Google AI Studio, Gemini for business, Google Antigravity and, for AI Pro and Ultra subscribers, the Gemini app, Google said. Google describes Neon as sitting between its larger Carbon model and its faster Hydrogen model, and 9to5Google reported that Neon replaces Argon in the Gemini app. VentureBeat reported that Neon's list prices are 50% higher than the mid-range models from Anthropic and OpenAI, while Google says Neon scores above both on that index. Details are in Google's announcement.

Why it matters: It extends a run of Gemini 4 releases over recent weeks and lands the day before Anthropic is set to begin trading on the Nasdaq.
