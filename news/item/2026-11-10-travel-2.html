Dulles Airport opens Concourse E, its first new concourse in nearly two decades

The concourse includes direct AeroTrain access, a food court and a nearly 40,000-square-foot United Club, United said. Each gate can handle domestic or international flights, and the added gates will let United fly more than 300 daily departures from Dulles next summer, the airline said. WTOP reported that construction began in November 2023.

It is the first of four planned Concourse E phases, which could add up to 36 more United gates and a new international customs area for connecting travelers, according to United.

Why it matters: WTOP quoted airport authority CEO Jack Potter as saying the concourse is the first step toward retiring Concourses C and D and eventually the airport's mobile lounges.
