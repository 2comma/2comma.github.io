California attorney general subpoenas Nvidia over whether chips or secrets reached China

Bonta said the subpoena will help the California Department of Justice evaluate whether Nvidia's actions violated state law, and said in a statement that the conduct of the company and its former CEO "raises bright red flags." TechCrunch reported that Nvidia's board has hired a law firm and an independent slate of directors to examine the matter, and that new chair Harvey Jones said this week the company does not plan to reinstate Huang. Details are in the attorney general's release.

Why it matters: It adds a state investigation to the scrutiny Nvidia has faced since Huang resigned last week.
