Mitsui Chemicals to buy chip materials maker JSR for 1.84 trillion yen with Development Bank of Japan

Mitsui Chemicals plans to take out a 1.64 trillion yen bridge loan, to be partly repaid with a share sale of up to 150 billion yen, Reuters reported, citing the filing. DBJ's stake will be converted into non-voting preferred shares. Kyodo said the company aims to complete the purchase in the second half of the fiscal year ending March 2028.

Shares of Mitsui Chemicals fell 8.5% on Monday, Reuters reported. Chief Executive Satoshi Ichimura said the deal is meant to accelerate the transformation of the company's business portfolio. JIC bought JSR for 900 billion yen in 2024, Reuters said.

Why it matters: JSR makes photoresists used to transfer circuit patterns onto semiconductor wafers, and Reuters said the deal is the largest in Japan's chemicals sector.
