Netflix acquires Joel Coen's 'Jack of Spades' for a fall 2027 theatrical release

Coen directed the film from a screenplay he wrote with Ethan Coen, Eleanor Catton and Conor McPherson, adapted from Frances Frederica Montrésor's 1901 novel "The Alien: A Story of Middle Age," according to the reports. It is set in the Scottish Highlands in the 1880s.

Variety said the deal followed a sales process it first reported in August. Netflix previously released the Coens' "The Ballad of Buster Scruggs" in 2018.

Why it matters: The deal adds a prestige title to Netflix's 2027 slate and continues its practice of giving some acquired films a theatrical run before streaming.
