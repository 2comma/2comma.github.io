Wisconsin reports infant measles death, state's first since 1990

As of November 3, Wisconsin had reported 431 measles cases in 13 counties in 2026, the highest total in more than 35 years, and nine people had been hospitalized, DHS said. The agency said it would release no further details about the infant or family to protect their privacy.

NBC News and CIDRAP reported that the Wisconsin death was the third measles-related death announced by a U.S. state this week, after cases in New York and Pennsylvania. CDC has confirmed 4,852 U.S. measles cases this year and four measles deaths in its official tally, while state health departments have reported eight measles-associated deaths, NBC News said.

Why it matters: DHS said measles can cause life-threatening complications, and two doses of the MMR vaccine are about 97% effective at preventing measles. CDC's national case count is the highest since 1991, according to NBC News.
