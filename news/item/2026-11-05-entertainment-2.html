Thursday · Nov 5, 2026 · Entertainment Digest · film-tv

Universal will re-release Nolan's The Odyssey on more than 1,000 screens starting Dec. 11

The film has grossed about $626 million domestically and $1.175 billion internationally, for about $1.80 billion worldwide, becoming Universal's and Nolan's highest-grossing film, the studio said. It opened July 17 and was the first feature filmed entirely in IMAX.

Deadline reported the re-release follows the film's mid-November Premium VOD, 4K and DVD release. Variety said the Dec. 11 run includes the IMAX 70mm format that drove much of the summer demand. The film remains Universal's highest-grossing international release after recently surpassing Furious 7, Universal said.

Why it matters: The return to more than 1,000 screens keeps a $1.8 billion hit in theaters after home video arrives, extending one of 2026's top box-office titles.
