Friday · Nov 6, 2026 · World Digest · elections

Israel's president to name a candidate to form a government on Sunday after 67 lawmakers back Eisenkot

Herzog made the announcement after two days of consultations with the parties elected to the 120-seat Knesset on October 27. He said the results of the consultations "appear clear", but that the law requires him to judge who is able to form a government, which is "not entirely clear" because of "mutual vetoes between the blocs and within the blocs themselves," as quoted by The Times of Israel. He plans personal talks with some party leaders over the weekend.

Al Jazeera reported that Eisenkot, like Netanyahu, has ruled out a government with the Arab-led parties, although both of those parties recommended him. United Torah Judaism recommended neither candidate, according to Ynet.

Why it matters: The October 27 election gave Netanyahu's bloc 53 seats, Eisenkot's alliance 48 and the Arab-led parties 19, according to Al Jazeera. The lawmaker Herzog picks will have 28 days to form a government, with a possible extension of up to 14 days, according to JNS.
