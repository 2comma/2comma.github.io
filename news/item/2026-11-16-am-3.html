Monday · Nov 16, 2026 · Morning Digest · economy

China industrial output slows to 4.8% in October; retail sales rise 0.4%

The bureau also said the urban surveyed unemployment rate was 5.1% in October, consumer prices rose 0.7% year on year and producer prices rose 5.7%. CNBC said analysts had forecast a 5.3% rise in industrial output and a 0.8% rise in retail sales, and a 7% decline in January-October fixed-asset investment. The official statement said the imbalance between strong supply and weak domestic demand remained acute.

Why it matters: The weaker-than-expected readings point to a deeper domestic slowdown in the world's second-largest economy.
