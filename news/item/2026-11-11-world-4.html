US charges three men over alleged Iran-backed attack plots, including one at a UK air base used by US forces

Prosecutors allege the men acted in the name of a group called Harakat Ashab al-Yamin al-Islamiya (HAYI), and FBI Director Kash Patel said they supported Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps and the Iran-backed Iraqi militia Kataib Hezbollah, the BBC reported. Those charged are Iranian national Mohsen Mohajeri, Iraqi national Mohammad Ali al-Hassani and Mohammad Baqer Saad Dawood al-Saadi, a dual Iranian-Iraqi citizen already held in New York on earlier charges.

The indictment alleges al-Hassani paid associates in Britain to survey the base and to drive three vans, some carrying barrels of fuel, toward it on September 26, according to the BBC. A major incident was declared near the base early on September 27; police found petrol but no explosives. Seven men have been arrested in Britain over the incident.

Why it matters: The Iranian embassy in London has rejected any link to the incident, the BBC reported. The charges are allegations, and none of the three has been convicted.
