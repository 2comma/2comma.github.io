Norse Atlantic files for bankruptcy and cancels all future flights

The airline told customers not to come to airports, including passengers stranded away from home, The Guardian reported. Its Norwegian operating subsidiary was placed into bankruptcy proceedings after failing to secure short-term funding, and the company said it was seeking a way to keep its UK unit operating, Reuters reported.

Norse flew low-cost long-haul routes from London Gatwick and the US to Europe and Asia; flights next month from Gatwick and Manchester to Thailand are among those scrapped, according to The Guardian.

Why it matters: Passengers holding Norse tickets now need refunds or rebooking, and the collapse shows how higher fuel costs this year are straining airlines.
