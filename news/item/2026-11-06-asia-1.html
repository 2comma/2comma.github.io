Friday · Nov 6, 2026 · Asia Digest · markets

Nikkei, KOSPI and Taiex plunge as fears of US curbs on AI data centers hit Asian chip stocks

The KOSPI plunged more than 8% at one point, Yonhap reported, and CNA said a circuit breaker suspended trading in Seoul. Kyodo reported that the Nikkei Stock Average closed down 3,643.13 points. Focus Taiwan said the Taiex fell below the 40,000-point mark and that the market recovered from its intraday low on hopes of government measures to support stocks, dealers said.

South Korea's Finance Minister Lee Hyoung-il said the government would act to prevent excessive market reactions and that the economy's fundamentals remain solid, Yonhap reported.

Why it matters: Nikkei Asia said the selloff followed a tech sell-off on Wall Street and investor concern about possible delays to data-center construction after the Democratic midterm victory.
