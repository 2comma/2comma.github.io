'Grand Theft Auto VI' reviews land at 99 on Metacritic ahead of its launch

The game launches worldwide on November 19, CBS News reported. Pre-launch sales are approaching $1.7 billion, with more than 17 million units sold, according to Sensor Tower data cited by The Hollywood Reporter.

Reviewers including IGN, Eurogamer and Bloomberg's Jason Schreier praised its scale and storytelling, the BBC reported. Rockstar has not disclosed the game's cost.

Why it matters: It is the first new main 'Grand Theft Auto' game in 13 years and one of the most anticipated entertainment releases of the year.
