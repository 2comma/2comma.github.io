Nvidia board reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO, effective immediately

Nvidia said the board acted after meetings with Huang and received input from senior levels of the US government, and that based on that input it is confident his return poses no national security risk. Colette Kress goes back to her role as chief financial officer after serving as interim CEO, and Harvey Jones remains board chair, the company said.

AP reported that the reversal came days after the board had rejected Huang's offer to return, and that President Donald Trump had called for his reinstatement. Trump told CNBC he spoke with the board and "convinced" them. Nvidia said an independent committee's investigation will continue and that it will hire a chief compliance officer.

Why it matters: Nvidia's chips power much of the AI industry, and its shares rose 31% on Monday after the announcement, CNBC reported.
