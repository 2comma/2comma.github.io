Monday · Nov 9, 2026 · Politics Digest · policy

Rep. Thomas Massie files impeachment articles against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, forcing a House vote

The 34-page resolution contains eight articles, three of which accuse Hegseth of waging war against Iran without congressional authorization, CBS News reported. PolitiFact said other articles cite the Caribbean boat strikes, a strike in Iran that hit an elementary school, actions against Sen. Mark Kelly, the capture of Venezuela's Nicolás Maduro and military action in Yemen.

Republicans could try to set the resolution aside with a procedural motion, and at least three Republicans would need to join all Democrats for it to pass, PolitiFact reported. House Democratic leader Hakeem Jeffries said he supports impeachment, while Hegseth called the effort partisan, according to PolitiFact. President Donald Trump has defended him.

Why it matters: The House has impeached only two Cabinet secretaries, William Belknap in 1876 and Alejandro Mayorkas in 2024, PolitiFact noted.
