IRS raises the 2027 401(k) contribution limit to $25,500; IRA limit stays at $7,500

The catch-up limit for workplace plan participants aged 50 and over rises to $8,500 from $8,000, allowing them to contribute up to $34,000, and a higher catch-up limit of $11,750 applies to those aged 60 to 63, the IRS said. The IRA catch-up limit for people 50 and over stays at $1,100.

The income phase-out range for Roth IRA contributions rises to $158,000 to $173,000 for single filers and heads of household, and to $250,000 to $260,000 for married couples filing jointly, according to the IRS. CNBC noted that the announcement came a week after the agency released other inflation adjustments, including income tax brackets.

Why it matters: The new limits take effect in 2027, the IRS said.
