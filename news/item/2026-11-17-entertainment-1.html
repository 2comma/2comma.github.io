Take-Two cuts about a third of staff at Civilization studio Firaxis

A spokesperson for Take-Two's publishing label said Firaxis is "restructuring and recentering around the studio's core strength" of strategy games and that the studio is being reduced to focus on design, technology and player experience, VGC reported, citing Bloomberg.

A new XCOM game in development has faced struggles, The Verge reported, citing Bloomberg.

Why it matters: Firaxis is one of the best-known strategy game studios, and the cuts add to a run of layoffs across the video game industry.
