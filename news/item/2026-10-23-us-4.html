Defense for man charged in Charlie Kirk killing seeks 2029 trial; prosecutors propose 2028

In filings ahead of a scheduling hearing Friday before Judge Tony Graf in Provo, Robinson's lawyers said a capital trial would last six to seven months and that they need time to review evidence and handle issues such as venue and jury selection. The Utah County Attorney's Office, which is seeking the death penalty, said it expects a 17-week trial and proposed Jan. 31, 2028, to avoid starting near the holidays.

Lawyers for Kirk's widow, Erika Kirk, called the defense request "grossly out of line" with comparable cases and asked for a trial next year, preferably by Oct. 4, according to Reuters. Robinson, 23, has pleaded not guilty to seven counts.

Why it matters: The date the judge sets will decide how long one of the country's most closely watched capital cases takes to reach a jury.
