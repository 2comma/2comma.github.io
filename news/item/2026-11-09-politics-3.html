Trump says his deportation policy 'might be too tough' after midterm losses

Trump said the crackdown "both helped and hurt" Republicans, CBS reported. He did not give specifics about a possible deal, AP reported, and shortly after taping the interview on Friday he named Stephen Miller, a key architect of his immigration agenda, as White House chief of staff.

Senate Democratic leader Chuck Schumer criticized the Miller appointment on social media, AP reported. Trump also said he may or may not get involved in the 2028 Republican presidential nomination contest.

Why it matters: CBS News said support for Republicans fell in South Texas and other heavily Latino areas in the midterms, a shift many voters linked at least in part to the immigration crackdown.
