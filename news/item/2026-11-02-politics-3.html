Former Chicago FBI chief sues Patel, Blanche and U.S. attorney, saying he was forced out over 'Broadview Six' case

The suit names Boutros, FBI Director Kash Patel, Attorney General Todd Blanche, the FBI and the Justice Department, according to the reports. It alleges that Boutros told department officials that DePodesta and his sister-in-law, a defense lawyer for one of the defendants, worked to undermine the case, which DePodesta denies, Fox 32 Chicago reported. DePodesta is seeking reinstatement, back pay and damages, and alleges violations of his First and Fifth Amendment rights.

The case involved six people charged after a September 2025 protest outside an immigration facility in Broadview, Ill.; all charges were later dropped. Boutros has denied that politics played any role in charging decisions, the Sun-Times reported.

Why it matters: The lawsuit puts allegations about how protest cases from the Chicago-area immigration enforcement operation were handled before a federal court.
