Monday · Nov 16, 2026 · World Digest · conflict

Ukrainian drones hit the Crimea bridge as Russia reports a barrage of more than 1,300 drones

Reuters said Russian defence ministry data showed it was the biggest overnight drone attack on Russia since the full-scale war began in 2022, with about 600 drones flying toward the Moscow region according to the city's mayor. Oil refineries were also targeted, Reuters and the BBC reported.

Traffic on the bridge was restricted after the strike, and a Russian deputy prime minister said repairs were being organized, Global News reported. Kyiv came under a record 17 air raid alerts in one day, and at least seven people were killed by Russian strikes, the BBC reported.

Why it matters: The 19-kilometre bridge is the only direct link between the transport networks of Russia and Crimea, and the strike came days before Trump, Putin and Xi are due to meet in China, AFP noted.
