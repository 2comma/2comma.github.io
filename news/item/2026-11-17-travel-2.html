Singapore Airlines jet returns to Rockhampton after second cabin smoke incident in a week

The plane was on a ferry flight with seven crew members and no passengers aboard, the airline told CNA. It had been cleared for service by ground engineers in Rockhampton, according to CNA.

On November 11, the same aircraft, operating flight SQ236 from Brisbane to Singapore, diverted to Rockhampton after cabin smoke was detected, and all passengers disembarked safely, The Straits Times reported. SIA said it is investigating the cause.

Why it matters: A repeat incident on the same jet raises questions about the earlier fix, and the cause has not been identified.
