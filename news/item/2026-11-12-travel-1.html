Thunderstorms and tornado warnings trigger ground stops at New York-area airports

The National Weather Service issued a tornado warning for Queens at 1:14 p.m. ET for a storm moving toward JFK, weather.com reported. Flash flooding near the airport forced parts of the JFK Expressway to close. A tornado was later confirmed in New Castle County, Delaware, only the second November tornado in the state since records began in 1950, according to weather.com.

The FAA's National Airspace System status page listed thunderstorm-related ground stops and ground delay programs at the three airports on Thursday evening.

Why it matters: Rain was expected to continue in the region into Friday, weather.com reported.
