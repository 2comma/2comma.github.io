Sunday · Nov 22, 2026 · Weekend Digest · public-health

118 children fall ill after eating noodles at a village fair in India's Madhya Pradesh

Forty-five of the children and all 12 adults were admitted to the Balaghat district hospital, while the rest of the children were discharged after first aid, the Indian Express reported. Officials said Sunday that all patients were stable, according to The Hindu, and food safety officials collected 13 samples from the vendor for testing.

Why it matters: The outbreak sickened more than 100 children at a single village event, and officials seized the vendor's stall pending laboratory tests, the Indian Express reported.
