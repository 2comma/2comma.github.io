Saturday · Nov 14, 2026 · Weekend Digest · aviation

FAA ground stop traced to copper-to-fiber upgrade by contractor L3Harris, Duffy says

Duffy said a newly connected router "flooded" other routers in the air traffic control network with over 1,000 connection attempts roughly every five seconds, disrupting aircraft communications and radar feeds, CNBC reported. He said investigators are still working out whether faulty hardware, a misconfiguration or contractor error was to blame. FAA Administrator Bryan Bedford said there was "zero evidence" of anything nefarious.

Nearly 25,000 flights were delayed and over 9,000 canceled on Friday, and more than 4,000 cancellations were logged Saturday morning, NPR reported, citing flight-tracking data. Bedford said schedules should be back to normal by Sunday evening.

Why it matters: It was the longest halt to US departures since 9/11, and officials' explanation points to aging air traffic control telecoms as a single point of failure.
