Iran's Revolutionary Guard says self-imposed missile range limit has been lifted

Brig. Gen. Hossein Mohebbi said the restriction "has been lifted for us," citing a directive from Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei to respond to threats without geographical limits, Tasnim and Iran International reported. Mohebbi said capabilities should be shown in practice rather than by statements, and AP also reported the Guard's claim.

Iran has long said its ballistic missiles were capped near 2,000 kilometers, enough to reach Israel and many U.S. bases in the region. An IRGC aerospace adviser separately said Saturday that Iran's missiles still had a range of about 2,000 kilometers, Iran International reported, underscoring that the announcement does not by itself prove a longer-range arsenal is fielded.

Why it matters: Any move beyond the previous range cap would expand the set of potential targets in a conflict that still threatens energy routes and regional security.
