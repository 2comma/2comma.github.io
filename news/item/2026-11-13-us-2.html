Brooklyn building collapse death toll reaches eight as New York City inspects related construction sites

The fire department ended search and rescue operations on Thursday afternoon and said there were no further victims, Gothamist reported. City buildings records show a stop-work order was issued at the adjacent lot in March over missing safety measures, and a later penalty for continuing work, Gothamist reported.

Mayor Zohran Mamdani said the five entities involved in the construction project are tied to about 100 other sites, which inspectors have begun sweeping, Gothamist reported. "We will hold them fully accountable," Mamdani said of anyone found responsible.

Why it matters: Gothamist called it one of the worst such disasters in recent city history; the cause is under investigation.
