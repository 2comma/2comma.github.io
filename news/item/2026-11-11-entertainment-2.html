Los Angeles' Radford Studio Center heads to a Nov. 24 foreclosure auction as Netflix pursues the lot

Netflix had been in talks to buy the lot for as much as $400 million, a fraction of the $1.85 billion Hackman Capital Partners paid for it in 2021, The Hollywood Reporter said. Lenders repossessed the property nine months ago, and they are seeking an estimated $1.3 billion, roughly the debt tied to it, the outlet reported.

"The deal is progressing on the agreed upon timetable and with the agreed upon sets of milestone dates," a Netflix spokesperson told The Hollywood Reporter. The outlet said a competing bidder could still emerge at the auction.

Why it matters: The Hollywood Reporter said owning the campus would let Netflix consolidate Los Angeles production it now spreads across rented stages, as filming in the region hovers near historic lows.
