Thursday · Nov 12, 2026 · World Digest · courts

UK jury convicts two Iranian men of spying on London-based journalists for Iran's Revolutionary Guards

Mostafa Sepahvand, 41, and Farhad Javadi Manesh, 46, were found guilty of conduct likely to assist the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, police said. Sepahvand was also convicted of surveillance intended to commit serious violence against a person in the UK; Manesh was acquitted of a similar charge. A third man, Shapoor Qalehali Khani Noori, 57, was acquitted of all charges.

The targets included two Iran International journalists, a lawyer for UK Lawyers for Israel and a media production professional, police said. Sepahvand admitted carrying out the surveillance but said he acted under duress, according to the CPS. AP reported that Britain outlawed the IRGC in July.

Why it matters: The two men are due to be sentenced at the Old Bailey on Dec. 17, police said.
