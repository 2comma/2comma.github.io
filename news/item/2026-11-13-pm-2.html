OpenAI launches ChatGPT Wallet so its Dots agents can buy items with user approval

Users aged 18 and older add an eligible US-issued card to the wallet, and the agent charges it with a payment token so card details are not shared with the agent or the merchant, OpenAI said, according to The Verge and TechCrunch. Every purchase requires approval with a security check such as Face ID or a fingerprint, The Verge reported.

OpenAI said it eventually expects users to be able to set spending limits, purchase categories or time periods and pre-approve purchases, TechCrunch reported. Some retailers restrict or block AI agents, The Verge noted.

Why it matters: The launch puts OpenAI directly into agent-driven shopping, with card-network tokenization handling payment details.
