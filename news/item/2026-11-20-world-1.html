Russian missiles land in Poland as Moscow strikes Kyiv's bridges and kills eight in Ukraine

The overnight strikes hit the three bridges across the Dnipro River in Kyiv and damaged energy facilities, according to Reuters and the BBC. President Volodymyr Zelenskiy said Russia launched more than 400 drones, Reuters reported, adding that the attack followed Putin's vow to retaliate for Ukrainian strikes on a bridge to Crimea.

AP reported that Russian missiles and a drone came down in southeastern Poland, and that Prime Minister Donald Tusk said the crash was likely an intentional provocation. CBS News also reported that Poland accused Russia of an "intentional provocation."

Why it matters: Poland is a NATO member, and Russian missiles landing on its soil raise the risk of the war drawing in the alliance.
