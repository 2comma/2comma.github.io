Pope Leo begins 12-day Latin America tour three weeks after lung cancer surgery

Vatican News reported that the papal plane left Rome's Fiumicino airport on Friday morning. The itinerary covers Montevideo, Paysandú and Florida in Uruguay, then Buenos Aires, Córdoba and Luján in Argentina, and Lima, Chiclayo, Cusco and Pucallpa in Peru. AP reported that the Vatican confirmed on October 26 that doctors had found no medical reasons preventing the trip.

The BBC reported that the pope's medical team said the cancer had not spread after the tumour and the lower lobe of his right lung were removed, and that the Vatican says no extraordinary measures are being taken for his health.

Why it matters: According to the BBC, the trip includes Uruguay's first papal visit in four decades and a return to Peru, where the pope spent almost 20 years, and the Vatican says he will meet survivors of clerical sexual abuse in all three countries.
