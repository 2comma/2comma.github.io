Stacy Keach, who played Mike Hammer on television and was a celebrated stage actor, dies at 85

Keach died "after a long and difficult battle with a serious heart condition and heart failure," his wife, Malgosia Tomassi Keach, said in a statement, according to The Hollywood Reporter. He starred in the 1972 boxing drama Fat City and in The New Centurions, played Sgt. Stedenko in the Cheech and Chong film Up in Smoke and co-wrote and starred in The Long Riders with his brother James, the outlets reported.

Trained at the Yale School of Drama and the London Academy of Music and Dramatic Art, Keach played Hamlet on stage several times and won a Golden Globe in 1989 for portraying Ernest Hemingway in a miniseries, The Hollywood Reporter said. His later work included the film Nebraska in 2013.

Why it matters: NPR said his career spanned more than half a century on stage and screen.
