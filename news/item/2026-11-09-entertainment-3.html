Monday · Nov 9, 2026 · Entertainment Digest · music

Rockstar and PlayStation announce a one-day Grand Theft Auto VI music festival in Miami

Tickets are offered through a pre-sale lottery on DICE that closes Tuesday, Nov. 10, and selected fans can buy up to two tickets on Nov. 11, Rolling Stone reported.

Grand Theft Auto VI goes on sale Nov. 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with its soundtrack album out the same day and an original score by Justice due Dec. 11, Rolling Stone and VGC reported.

Why it matters: Rockstar said the event will feature artists from the game and its official soundtrack, tying the launch to a live concert, VGC reported.
