French national strike cancels one in 10 TGV trains and three Eurostar services

SNCF said regional TER trains would be heavily disrupted in Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, Brittany, Occitanie, Hauts-de-France and parts of Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur, with some disruption lasting into Friday morning, BFM Business reported, citing AFP. In the Paris region, RER D was set to run one train in three and RER C one in two, while RER A and lines K, P and V were expected to run normally.

The four representative rail unions, CGT, Unsa ferroviaire, Sud Rail and CFDT, called the 24-hour strike to join the national unions' call over wages and jobs, according to BFM Business.

Why it matters: Travelers in or through France faced reduced rail service on Thursday, and SNCF told passengers to check line-by-line forecasts on its apps before going to the station, BFM Business reported.
