US says it downed two Iranian drones headed for Bahrain after Iran claims a strike on a US radar

US Central Command said there were no injuries or damage, AP reported. Iranian state media cited the Revolutionary Guard as saying the attack was retaliation for what it described as a US strike on a telecommunications tower in Sirik county in southern Iran, according to Reuters as published by Al-Monitor.

It was Iran's first claim of targeting a US military asset in a Gulf country since early September, Reuters reported.

Why it matters: The exchange comes as the US military continues to build up its presence in the Middle East, NPR reported.
