US proposes new Section 301 tariffs on 14 trading partners, with a 20% rate on China

The duties follow an investigation under Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974 and are still subject to public comment, so they could change before taking effect. CNBC reported that the rates would not exceed those set in the framework trade deals already agreed with each partner, and Reuters reported that a senior US official said final action could come as early as January. Goods from the EU, Japan, South Korea and Switzerland would face 15%, which could rise to 20%, Reuters reported. Details are in CNBC's report.

Why it matters: The plan is the administration's latest attempt to rebuild its broad tariff program after the Supreme Court struck down its emergency-powers tariffs in February.
