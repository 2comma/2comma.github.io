Brazil's B3 and six major banks plan Open BRL, a real-pegged stablecoin, for early 2027

B3 will run the technology at first while the partner institutions build integrations and test uses for it, according to B3's announcement. Luiz Masagão, a B3 vice president, told Valor Econômico that the platform is expected to start operating in the first quarter of 2027, that each token will be backed by 1 real held in reserve, and that other institutions can join from launch, CoinDesk and Cointelegraph reported. B3 also plans to use the token to settle trades in tokenized shares.

Why it matters: A shared token run by the country's main exchange would give rival Brazilian banks a common settlement asset in a stablecoin market dominated by dollar-pegged coins.
