Putin places more Japan Tobacco assets in Russia under local control

A presidential decree published Friday placed all shares in Pereslavl-Tobacco, JTI Innovative Products and JTI Russia under temporary management of a Russian company, Kyodo reported, citing Tass. The earlier Thursday decree targeted manufacturing operations of JT International, drawing protests from the Japanese government, Kyodo and Nikkei Asia reported.

Moscow said the actions follow a 2023 executive order allowing assets of entities from "unfriendly countries" to be placed under Russian control in response to sanctions over the war in Ukraine, Kyodo reported. Japan Tobacco has continued operating in Russia even as many Japanese firms withdrew, the reports said.

Why it matters: The seizures add commercial pressure on one of the few large Japanese companies still active in Russia as Tokyo-Moscow ties deteriorate.
