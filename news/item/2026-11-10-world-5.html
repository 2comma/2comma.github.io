Clashes in Bangladesh as supporters of ousted leader Sheikh Hasina demand her return

The protests began a day after Hasina said she had asked for a passport to return to Bangladesh in December, according to the BBC. The government said it would not issue her one, and a state prosecutor said a death sentence handed down in absentia last year would be carried out if she returned, the BBC reported. Hasina has denied the charges.

A 60-year-old bus driver died of smoke inhalation after a vehicle was set on fire in Faridpur, and a police vehicle was burned in Gopalganj, the BBC said. A senior leader of the student wing told the BBC its supporters were not responsible for the violence. The Associated Press reported that police linked crude bomb attacks over three days to paid recruits tied to a banned group.

Why it matters: The BBC described the demonstrations as the first organized show of force by Hasina's supporters since she was ousted in August 2024.
