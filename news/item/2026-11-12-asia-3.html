Singapore bars former Megaspeed chief Alice Huang from re-entering after finding she gave police inaccurate information

The ministry said police and Singapore Customs began investigating Megaspeed and other companies in February 2025 over suspected false declarations in the purchase of servers that were exported through Singapore to Malaysia. Police were examining whether the servers were later routed elsewhere, contrary to the declarations, the ministry said. Huang, a Hong Kong resident, was not investigated for espionage, it added.

The ministry said police sought help from US authorities in obtaining information from server supplier Supermicro but have received limited assistance. Investigations into Megaspeed and six other people are continuing. Megaspeed CEO James Tan said the company is committed to working with the US government, according to CNA.

Why it matters: Singapore has charged four individuals and four companies since February 2025 over fraud linked to server purchases, the ministry said, adding that it does not condone companies using Singapore to circumvent other countries' export controls.
