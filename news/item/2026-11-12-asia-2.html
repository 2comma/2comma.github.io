Taiwan names Acer founder Stan Shih as APEC envoy and says it will join the China-hosted summit on an 'equal basis'

China's Foreign Ministry said late on Wednesday that it handles the "Taiwan region's" participation in APEC in line with the one-China principle and relevant APEC memorandums of understanding, and that Taiwan's representatives can attend "as normal" if those practices are followed, Reuters reported. Presidential Office spokeswoman Karen Kuo said Taiwan would ask for an immediate correction if unfair treatment occurs, the Taipei Times reported.

Lai said he wants Shih to convey that Taiwan will remain a stable and trustworthy force as the global economy faces challenges from geopolitics, energy and supply chain restructuring. Focus Taiwan reported that Lai also asked Shih to convey Taiwan's interest in AI cooperation.

Why it matters: APEC is one of the few international bodies that include Taiwan, which China claims as its territory, Reuters reported. The leaders' meeting is set for Nov. 18-19.
