Toledo lifts evacuation orders after chemical leak; mayor blames employee error

Toledo Fire & Rescue lifted the last evacuation zone around MC Tank Terminal at noon Friday and turned the site over to the company and the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency for cleanup, the city said. Hazmat crews found no detectable hazards on re-entry after the tank, which held glycidyl methacrylate, finished venting late Thursday.

Mayor Wade Kapszukiewicz said an employee's handling of the tank's temperature while trying to transfer the liquid caused a chemical reaction, and that the company had accepted responsibility. Hundreds of people were forced from their homes after the leak began Tuesday night, and 57 were treated at hospitals, the AP reported. The investigation is continuing.

Why it matters: The city is advising people in the former 3-mile shelter-in-place zone to throw away produce that was outdoors, and it plans to clean playgrounds there.
