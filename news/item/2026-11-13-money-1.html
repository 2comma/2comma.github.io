Family Dollar agrees to $63 million settlement over charging more at the register than shelf prices

Under the proposed order, Family Dollar must set up a $40 million fund for eligible customers and a separate $20 million discount program, and pay the states $3 million, CBS News reported. Eligible customers include anyone who bought from a Family Dollar store from Jan. 1, 2023, to Oct. 1, 2026, according to CBS News.

The retailer must also run a pricing accuracy program that is independently assessed and certified each year, the Illinois attorney general's office said. Family Dollar, which operates more than 7,000 stores, did not immediately respond to CBS News.

Why it matters: The FTC and 20 states sued Dollar General in October over similar pricing allegations, and that case is ongoing, CBS News reported.
