34 foreign airlines seek to delay moving to Vietnam's new Long Thanh airport

Twenty-four carriers want to delay the move until the winter 2027 schedule and 10 until summer 2028 or later, The Straits Times and VnExpress reported. The airlines say they need six to 12 months to adjust flight networks, assign aircraft and crew and set up ground services.

The airport near Ho Chi Minh City is meant to relieve congestion at Tan Son Nhat airport, The Straits Times reported.

Why it matters: The delays could leave Vietnam's flagship airport with few international flights when it opens.
