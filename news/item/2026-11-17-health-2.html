WHO says Congo's Ebola outbreak has more than doubled to over 11,000 cases

Opening the third meeting of WHO's emergency committee on the outbreak, Tedros said cases span 67 health zones in eight provinces and that children account for more than a quarter of confirmed cases and a third of deaths. Between 400 and 500 new cases are still reported each week, he said.

He also cited progress, saying rapid transmission has been halted in most early epicentres and about 3,000 people have recovered, UN News reported.

Why it matters: Six months after it was declared an international health emergency, the outbreak is still expanding in some areas, WHO said, and Kenya and Nigeria have reported imported cases.
