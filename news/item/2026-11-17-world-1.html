Tuesday · Nov 17, 2026 · World Digest · conflict

Section of the Kerch Bridge collapses after Ukrainian strike as Russian missiles hit two Kyiv bridges

The General Staff said the road link is out of service and the railway section is still standing with a damaged arch, AP and Interfax-Ukraine reported. President Volodymyr Zelenskiy called the strike the final stage of a weekslong operation that first targeted air defenses, according to AP.

In Kyiv, Russian ballistic missiles struck the North and South bridges over the Dnipro River, halting traffic, and Russia's army confirmed it hit both bridges, CBS News reported.

Why it matters: The Kerch Bridge is the main road and rail link between Russia and Crimea and a key supply route for Russian forces, AP noted.
