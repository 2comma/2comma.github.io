Singapore and Malaysia to raise cross-border taxi quota to 500 each from Nov. 16

CDG Zig of Singapore and Malaysia's Kumpool will join GrabCar as licensed operators under the Cross-Border Taxi Scheme, according to the joint statement. CNA reported that the larger quota lets the scheme accommodate up to 1,000 licensed taxis from both countries, compared with a combined 600 now.

The move follows enhancements introduced in May, including more flexible drop-off points in Singapore and in designated areas of Johor, CNA reported. The National Taxi Association said the quota doubling within a year has left members concerned about jobs and earnings, and has asked the Land Transport Authority to issue the extra licences in phases.

Why it matters: The ministries said the changes aim to give commuters and drivers more options for point-to-point trips between the two countries.
