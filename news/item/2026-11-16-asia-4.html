Thai court rejects Thaksin's bid to halt asset seizures in a 17 billion baht tax case

The court said the department's action was enforcement rather than a pre-judgment measure, so Thaksin lacked standing to seek the injunction, The Nation reported. His challenge over what he says is duplicate collection remains pending, with a pre-trial hearing set for Dec. 21, according to the report.

Bloomberg Tax put the claim at about $516 million.

Why it matters: The ruling lets the Revenue Department continue seizing assets while Thaksin's wider challenge proceeds.
