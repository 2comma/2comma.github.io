Google will replace Assistant with Gemini on its smart speakers and displays on Aug. 2, 2027

Google also said the Nest app will stop working on Jan. 25, 2027, and Nest devices will be managed in the Google Home app instead, according to The Verge. The Verge reported that Google will begin deleting Nest account data on March 25, 2027.

Android Authority reported that Google posted the cutoff on its Home and Nest community forums and that Gemini will then be the only voice assistant on its home hardware.

Why it matters: Owners of Nest and Google Home devices will need to move to the Home app and Gemini, completing Google's shift away from Assistant after it was retired on phones earlier this year, The Verge reported.
