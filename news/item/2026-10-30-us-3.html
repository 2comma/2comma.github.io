Kentucky appeals judge denies NCAA bid to block about 30 athletes from playing

"The NCAA has not shown that there is an imminent, ruinous, and incalculable injury that will befall it if this Court does not grant a stay," Combs wrote, according to Kentucky Today. A Jefferson County judge issued the injunction on Oct. 16.

A three-judge panel of the appeals court will consider the NCAA's request for further relief, and the athletes' lawyers have until Nov. 6 to respond, Kentucky Today reported. The athletes include Louisville basketball player Seth Trimble, AP reported.

Why it matters: The ruling keeps the players eligible as the college basketball season begins, while the broader dispute over NCAA eligibility limits continues in courts.
