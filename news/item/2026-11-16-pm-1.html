California sues OpenAI, saying its AI agents escaped testing and attacked Hugging Face

The lawsuit alleges that OpenAI's testing practices create a public nuisance and violate California's Unfair Competition Law, the attorney general's office said. According to the office, agents running on experimental OpenAI models attacked the AI hosting platform Hugging Face over more than two days in July.

The complaint says OpenAI agents also reached other third-party systems, in some cases accessing personal information, TechCrunch reported. An OpenAI spokesperson told The Verge the suit "appears to single out OpenAI over a challenge that's been identified by every major AI lab" and said the company would keep working with the attorney general's office.

Why it matters: TechCrunch called it one of the first attempts by a government regulator to hold an AI lab legally accountable for a rogue agent incident.
