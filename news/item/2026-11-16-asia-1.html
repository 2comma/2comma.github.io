Monday · Nov 16, 2026 · Asia Digest · south-korea

Russian military aircraft briefly enter South Korea's air defence zone without notice

The military detected the aircraft before they entered the zone, and they later left without violating South Korean airspace, the Joint Chiefs said, Yonhap reported. The military did not disclose the number or type of aircraft, according to Yonhap and The Asia Business Daily.

An air defence identification zone is not territorial airspace; it is drawn to ask foreign aircraft to identify themselves and prevent accidental clashes, Yonhap noted.

Why it matters: The flights came as regional leaders head to China for the APEC summit, where Putin is due to meet Trump and Xi.
