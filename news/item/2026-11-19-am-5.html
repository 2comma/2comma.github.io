Singapore issues S$100 million of tokenized central bank bills settled in wholesale CBDC

Five primary dealers, DBS, HSBC, OCBC, UOB and Standard Chartered, took part in the auction, which drew applications of more than twice the amount offered, MAS said, and the bills mature on Dec. 4. MAS said it plans to scale up issuance, add more institutions and widen use of the bills as collateral in live trials in 2027, and that the test network will be renamed SG Digital Ledger. CoinDesk and The Block also reported the issuance. Details are in MAS's announcement.

Why it matters: It puts a central bank debt instrument natively on a shared ledger, a step toward using tokenized assets as collateral in interbank lending.
