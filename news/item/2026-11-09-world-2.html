EU and E3 to join US-brokered Russia-Ukraine talks in Abu Dhabi despite Kremlin objections

Commission President Ursula von der Leyen said on Monday that "the EU will be present alongside our partners" after speaking with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy, Euronews reported. NPR said France's foreign minister announced on Sunday that European officials would attend, days after Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov ruled out a European role. According to Euronews, it is not clear whether the Europeans will meet the Russian delegation directly.

US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner will represent Washington, Euronews and the Guardian reported. Russia's team is led by Putin aide Vladimir Medinsky and Ukraine's by Rustem Umerov, according to Euronews. Secretary of State Marco Rubio said on Sunday that some progress had been made on postwar security arrangements, the Guardian reported.

Why it matters: AP and NPR said these are the first talks among Russian, Ukrainian and US negotiators since February, and AP reported that security guarantees top the agenda. Earlier rounds produced little visible progress, according to AP and NPR.
