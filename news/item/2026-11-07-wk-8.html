US measles cases top 5,182, highest annual total in 35 years, CDC data show

About 96% of cases were among people who were unvaccinated or whose vaccination status was unknown, ABC News reported, citing CDC data. Eight measles-associated deaths have been confirmed so far, including six in Pennsylvania and one each in New York and Wisconsin; the CDC has confirmed four of the eight and said the number is subject to change.

Eighteen cases involved international visitors, and about 11% of cases resulted in hospitalization, ABC News reported. Kindergarten MMR coverage was 92.4% in the 2025-26 school year, below the 95.2% rate in 2019-20, according to the same report.

Why it matters: Crossing the 5,000-case mark underscores how far vaccination coverage has slipped from the levels that once kept measles rare in the United States.
