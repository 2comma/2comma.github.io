Godzilla Minus Zero roars to $6.7 million in North American previews

Variety said the Takashi Yamazaki sequel is the first Japanese movie filmed for Imax and is expected to lead the weekend over Warner Bros.' The Cat in the Hat, which made about $1 million in previews and was forecast near $20 million. Deadline reported the $6.7 million preview haul beat several prior genre comps, including Predator: Badlands and Godzilla: King of the Monsters preview totals, and said a $30 million opening now looks like the low end of expectations.

Godzilla Minus One opened to about $11.4 million domestically three years ago and went on to about $57 million in North America and $113 million globally, Variety said. Deadline also noted Sony's Indian epic Ramayana: Rise of a Legend made about $3.5 million from its Thursday opening and Wednesday previews across 1,433 locations.

Why it matters: Strong preview grosses put a Japanese live-action sequel on track for one of the bigger November openings and a sharp step up from its Oscar-winning predecessor.
