'The Year of the Ghost' wins top prize at Amsterdam's IDFA documentary festival

The Chinese-born filmmaker, also known as Louis Liu, also shared the competition's Best Editing award with co-editor Chris van Oers, Deadline reported. The film uses archival footage, animation and family conversations to examine how modern Chinese history affects women's lives, according to the festival program. 'Memories of Lightning' was also among the top winners, Variety and The Hollywood Reporter reported.

Why it matters: IDFA is the world's largest documentary festival, and its winners often go on to awards campaigns.
