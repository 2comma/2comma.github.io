Mon Nov 16regulation

US issues rule requiring licenses for remote AI-chip access above 12 million TPP

The Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security released an interim final rule on Monday that requires a license when advanced computing items are destined for an overseas data center that will provide more than 12 million aggregate TPP of computing power to companies headquartered in Macau or Country Group D:5. The rule also adds license requirements for advanced semiconductor shipments to Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil, which it says present a higher-than-normal risk of diversion, and it formally rescinds the January 2025 AI Diffusion Rule.
